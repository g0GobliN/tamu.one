## [en] A performance with a social setting

By midnight the courtyard is full and nobody is watching in silence. A lamp is set where it will not be knocked, the singers sit along one wall, the drummers find their place, and the dancers come out into the middle of a space that is also somebody's kitchen yard, with the grain store on one side and the buffalo tied at the other. People talk, get up, come back, share food, doze against a pillar and wake when the drum changes.

That is the setting Pirkko Moisala describes from 1975, in a Gurung mountain village: a guru leading male singers, madal drummers and three teenage girl dancers, with the performance continuing through the night in a crowded household courtyard. The setting is the point rather than the background. Ghatu is a social event as well as a musical form, and the audience, hosts and performers share one space rather than facing each other across a stage — which changes what the performance is, because a dance watched from three feet away by people who will feed you afterwards is judged differently from a show.

Her research is grounded in that village and explicitly avoids claiming that all Gurungs share the same practices, a caution worth carrying through everything below. What follows is an account of a tradition as documented in particular places, not a national template.

The first distinction to make is between the full performance and the excerpt.

Hosting is part of the form. Somebody has to clear the yard, feed the singers, keep the lamp lit and find room for the neighbours, and the household that hosts a Ghatu is making a statement about its standing as well as doing the dancers a service.

A night is a long time, and the form is built for it: sections begin and end, the singing changes, the dancing pauses, and the audience's attention is allowed to drift and return without the performance collapsing.

## [ne] सामाजिक परिवेश भएको प्रस्तुति

मध्यरातमा आँगन भरिएको हुन्छ र कसैले पनि चुपचाप हेरिरहेको हुँदैन। एउटा बत्ती त्यहाँ राखिन्छ जहाँ ढुक्कुर लाग्दैन, गायकहरू एउटा भित्ताको छेउमा बस्छन्, मादलवालाहरू आफ्नो ठाउँ भेट्छन्, र नर्तकहरू बीचको खुला ठाउँमा आउँछन् — त्यो पनि कसैको भान्साको आँगन हो, एउटा छेउमा अन्नको भकारी र अर्को छेउमा बाँधिएको भैँसी। मानिस बोल्छन्, उठ्छन्, फर्केर आउँछन्, खाना बाँड्छन्, खम्बामा टाँसिएर निदाउँछन् र मादलको ताल फेरिएपछि ब्युँझन्छन्।

यही परिवेश Pirkko Moisala ले सन् १९७५ मा एउटा गुरुङ पहाडी गाउँमा देखेकी हुन्: गुरुको नेतृत्वमा पुरुष गायक, मादल बजाउने, र तीन किशोरी नर्तक, र रातभर चल्ने प्रस्तुति भीडभाड भएको घरको आँगनमा। परिवेश यहाँ पृष्ठभूमि होइन, मुख्य कुरा हो। घाटु संगीतको रूपजत्तिकै सामाजिक घटना पनि हो, र दर्शक, आयोजक तथा कलाकार एउटै ठाउँ बाँड्छन्, मञ्चको दुई छेउमा बसेर एकअर्कालाई हेर्दैनन् — जसले प्रस्तुतिको अर्थ फेर्छ, किनकि तीन फिट परबाट हेर्ने र पछि खुवाउने मानिसको सामु नाच्नु बेग्लै कुरा हो।

उनको अनुसन्धान त्यही गाउँमा आधारित छ र सबै गुरुङको अभ्यास एउटै हो भन्ने दाबी जानाजान गर्दैन, र त्यो होसियारी तलका सबै कुरामा लागू हुन्छ। यो कुनै ठाउँमा अभिलेख भएको परम्पराको वर्णन हो, राष्ट्रिय ढाँचा होइन।

आयोजना पनि रूपको भाग हो। कसैले आँगन सफा गर्नुपर्छ, गायकलाई खुवाउनुपर्छ, बत्ती बालिराख्नुपर्छ र छिमेकीलाई ठाउँ मिलाउनुपर्छ, र घाटु आयोजना गर्ने घरले आफ्नो हैसियतबारे पनि भनाइ राख्छ। पहिलो भेद भने पूरा प्रस्तुति र त्यसको अंशबीचको हो।

रात लामो हुन्छ, र रूप त्यसैका लागि बनेको छ: खण्ड सुरु हुन्छन् र टुङ्गिन्छन्, गाइने फेरिन्छ, नाच रोकिन्छ, र दर्शकको ध्यान भुल्न र फर्कन पाउँछ — प्रस्तुति भाँचिएर होइन।

पहिलो पटक हेर्नेलाई सबैभन्दा अनौठो लाग्ने कुरा यही हो: प्रस्तुति सुरु हुँदा कसैले "सुरु भयो" भन्दैन। गुरुले एउटा खण्ड थाल्छन्, मादलले समात्छ, र कोठा आफैँ शान्त हुन्छ।

## [en] Sati and baramase forms

Moisala distinguishes the complete annual performance, called sari or sati Ghatu in different transcriptions, from baramase performances that present selected episodes at weddings, visits and other celebrations. The two are related the way a whole book is related to a chapter read aloud, and confusing them has consequences: the annual performance carries obligations the excerpt does not, and the excerpt can travel to places the full observance cannot.

Her account places the full narrative at Baisakh Purnima, around the spring full moon, and links the occasion to divine favour and the rice crop. Harrison and Macfarlane's notes also describe opening observances near Shree Panchami and the complete performance at Baisakh Purnima. The calendar is therefore not a single date but a season with a beginning, and the agricultural logic is not decorative: a rite that asks for divine favour and concerns the rice crop belongs to the weeks when the crop's fate is being decided.

These accounts stay locally attributed rather than compressed into one fixed Gregorian date or duration, and that restraint is deliberate. A festival tied to a full moon and to agricultural work moves with both, and a book that printed one date would be wrong in most villages in most years. What a reader needs is the sequence and the reason for it, not a date that looks precise and travels badly.

The story the dancers carry through that sequence is a royal one.

An excerpt performed at a wedding is not a shortened version of the annual rite but a different social act: it fits into somebody else's celebration, in a hall or a yard in town, and it carries the story without the obligation that belongs to the full observance.

Baisakh Purnima and Shree Panchami are fixed points in a year that is otherwise determined by the crop, and the two observances are the outer frame of a season rather than a single evening's event.

## [ne] सती र बारामासे रूप

Moisala ले पूरा वार्षिक प्रस्तुति — फरक लेखाइमा सारि वा सती घाटु — लाई बारामासे प्रस्तुतिबाट छुट्याउँछिन्, जसमा विवाह, भेटघाट र अरू उत्सवमा छानिएका प्रसंग मात्र देखाइन्छ। दुईको सम्बन्ध पूरै किताब र त्यसको एउटा अध्याय सुनाउने सम्बन्धजस्तो हो, र दुवैलाई एउटै ठान्दा परिणाम हुन्छ: वार्षिक प्रस्तुतिले बोक्ने दायित्व अंशले बोक्दैन, र अंश त्यहाँ पुग्न सक्छ जहाँ पूरा विधि पुग्दैन।

उनको विवरणले पूरा कथा बैशाख पूर्णिमामा राख्छ, वसन्तको पूर्ण चन्द्रवरपर, र त्यो अवसरलाई दैवी कृपा तथा धानबालीसँग जोड्छ। Harrison र Macfarlane का टिप्पणीले पनि श्रीपञ्चमीनिर सुरुको विधि र बैशाख पूर्णिमामा पूरा प्रस्तुति वर्णन गर्छन्। त्यसैले पात्रो एउटा मिति होइन, सुरुवात भएको ऋतु हो, र कृषि-तर्क सजावट होइन: दैवी कृपा माग्ने र धानसँग सम्बन्धित विधि त्यही हप्तामा पर्छ जब बालीको भाग्य तय हुँदै हुन्छ।

यी विवरण स्थानीय रूपमा जोडिएका छन्, एउटै ग्रेगोरियन मिति वा अवधिमा खुम्च्याइएका होइनन्, र त्यो संयम जानाजान हो। पूर्ण चन्द्र र कृषि-कामसँग बाँधिएको चाड दुवैसँग सर्छ, र एउटै मिति छाप्ने किताब धेरै गाउँमा धेरै वर्ष गलत हुन्छ। पाठकलाई चाहिने कुरा क्रम र त्यसको कारण हो, ठ्याक्कै देखिने तर नमिल्ने मिति होइन।

विवाहमा देखाइने अंश फेरि फरक सामाजिक कर्म हो: त्यो अरू कसैको उत्सवभित्र पस्छ, सहरको हल वा आँगनमा, र कथालाई बोक्छ तर पूरा विधिको दायित्व बोक्दैन।

बैशाख पूर्णिमा र श्रीपञ्चमी वर्षका दुई स्थिर बिन्दु हुन्, जो बालीले तय गर्ने बाँकी वर्षलाई घेर्छन्; त्यसैले यी दुई विधि एउटा साँझको कार्यक्रम होइन, ऋतुको बाहिरी घेरा हुन्।

छोटो प्रसंगले पनि पूरा कथाको स्वाद दिन्छ, त्यसैले विवाहमा घाटु देख्ने पाहुनाले वार्षिक विधि देखेको छु भन्नु हुँदैन — उसले एउटा ढोका देखेको हो, घर होइन।

## [en] A royal story, with local names

The story the dancers carry is royal. In Moisala's account it concerns King Pashuram, also rendered Pasram, Queen Yamavati and their son; Harrison and Macfarlane record forms such as Parasuram and Yampawoti, while an older account gives Satyawati. The names drift between sources, and the drift is informative: it shows a narrative transmitted by performance rather than fixed by print.

The narrative includes the king's death and the queen's immolation — the motif known as sati — and this is the place where a reader is most likely to draw a wrong conclusion. Telling or dancing that story is not proof that Gurung communities practised widow immolation, and it is not proof that the characters are independently verified historical rulers. A community can carry a story about a queen who chose to die without that story describing its own marriage customs, in the same way that a European ballad about a king does not describe the constitution.

Names and interpretations vary with the performance and its documentation, and the variation should be kept visible rather than resolved. What is stable is the shape of the story: a king, a queen, a son, a death, and a consequence. Around that shape the performance arranges the material of ordinary life, which is what makes it more than a legend.

The royal frame is not where the dancers spend most of their night.

The drift between Pashuram and Parasuram, Yamavati and Yampawoti, is the ordinary life of a story carried by voice. A printed text fixes one spelling; a performed one changes with the mouth that learned it, and the variation is evidence of transmission rather than of error.

Keeping the variants is not pedantry. A family that knows the queen as Satyawati is not reciting a corrupted version of a text that says Yamavati; it is carrying a version, and versions are what oral traditions consist of.

An older account that gives the queen as Satyawati is not necessarily later or worse informed; it may simply come from a family or a village that kept a different version of the same marriage.

## [ne] स्थानीय नाम भएको राजकीय कथा

नर्तकले बोक्ने कथा राजकीय हो। Moisala को विवरणमा त्यो राजा पशुराम, जसलाई पासराम पनि भनिन्छ, रानी यमावती र तिनका छोरासँग सम्बन्धित छ; Harrison र Macfarlane ले पारसुराम र याम्पावोतीजस्ता रूप लेख्छन्, जबकि एउटा पुरानो विवरणले सत्यवती दिन्छ। नाम सर्नु आफैँमा सूचना हो: यो कथा प्रिन्टले स्थिर गरेको होइन, प्रस्तुतिले बोकेको हो।

कथामा राजाको मृत्यु र रानीको जीवितै जल्ने प्रसंग छ — सती भनेर चिनिने प्रेरणा — र यही ठाउँमा पाठकले सबैभन्दा ठूलो गलत निष्कर्ष निकाल्न सक्छ। त्यो कथा भन्नु वा नाच्नु गुरुङ समुदायले विधवा जलाउने चलन पाल्यो भन्ने प्रमाण होइन, न ती पात्र प्रमाणित ऐतिहासिक शासक हुन्। समुदायले आफ्नो विवाह-चलन वर्णन नगर्ने कथा बोक्न सक्छ, जसरी युरोपेली लोकगीतमा राजाको कथा हुँदैमा त्यो संविधानको वर्णन हुँदैन।

नाम र व्याख्या प्रस्तुति तथा अभिलेखअनुसार फरक पर्छ, र त्यो भिन्नता मेटाउनुभन्दा देखिने गरी राख्नु राम्रो। स्थिर कुरा कथाको आकार हो: राजा, रानी, छोरा, मृत्यु र त्यसको परिणाम। त्यही आकारवरपर प्रस्तुतिले दैनिक जीवनको सामग्री मिलाउँछ, र त्यसैले यो किंवदन्तीभन्दा बढी हुन्छ।

राजकीय ढाँचा भने नर्तकले रात बिताउने ठाउँ होइन।

रूपहरू जोगाउनु शास्त्रीयता होइन। जसको परिवारले रानीलाई सत्यवती भन्छ, उसले यमावती भन्ने पाठको बिग्रेको संस्करण पढिरहेको छैन; उसले एउटा संस्करण बोकिरहेको छ, र मौखिक परम्परा संस्करणहरूकै बन्छ।

तीन नाम भेट्दा पाठक अलमलिनु स्वाभाविक हो, तर अलमल नै सही अवस्था हो: एउटै कथाका धेरै नाम हुनु भनेको कथा धेरै ठाउँमा बाँचेको प्रमाण हो।

## [en] Everyday life within the narrative

Within the royal narrative sit episodes of ordinary work: flowers, rice cultivation, hunting, fishing, marriage, battle and a hermit's life. The sequence is not a pageant of kingship. It is a set of scenes in which the audience recognises its own labour, and the recognition is part of the pleasure.

In Moisala's account the rice-farming episode follows the process from preparing the field and planting to eating the crop — a whole agricultural year compressed into movement and song. A woman who has spent the morning transplanting sees her work danced that night, and the dance does not merely illustrate the work; it orders it, gives it a beginning and an end, and makes it something worth carrying across generations.

Such sequences tie a royal story to recognisable labour and relationships, and the study treats performance as cultural knowledge: movement and music can communicate ways of understanding a world rather than simply illustrate a text. That is the researcher's analysis of a particular tradition, not a universal explanation supplied by every performer. A dancer may say she is dancing a story; an elder may say she is dancing the year. Both are describing the same sequence from different sides.

The knowledge is not only in the words. It is also in how the body moves, and that is the hardest part to write down.

The episodes also order the year for the audience. Hunting, fishing, transplanting and harvesting are not random scenes but a calendar, and watching them in sequence is one way a village reminds itself what comes next and what it owes to the season.

The hermit's episode is the quietest and, for many audiences, the most affecting: a life withdrawn from the village, danced in front of the village, and understood by everyone who has an uncle or a grandfather who went that way.

## [ne] कथाभित्रको दैनिक जीवन

राजकीय कथाभित्र दैनिक कामका प्रसंग पर्छन्: फूल, धान खेती, सिकार, माछा मार्ने, विवाह, युद्ध र सन्यासीको जीवन। यो राजकीयताको झाँकी होइन। यो दृश्यहरूको शृङ्खला हो जसमा दर्शकले आफ्नै श्रम चिन्दछ, र त्यो चिनाइ नै आनन्दको भाग हो।

Moisala को विवरणमा धान खेतीको प्रसंग बारी तयार गरेर रोप्नेदेखि बाली खानेसम्मको पूरा प्रक्रिया पछ्याउँछ — एउटा कृषि-वर्ष गतिले र गीतले खुम्च्याएर। बिहान रोपाइँ गरेकी महिलाले रातमा आफ्नै काम नाचिएको देख्छिन्, र नाचले त्यो कामलाई केवल देखाउँदैन; यसलाई क्रम दिन्छ, सुरु र अन्त्य दिन्छ, र पुस्तौँसम्म बोक्नलायक बनाउँछ।

यस्ता शृङ्खलाले राजकीय कथालाई चिनजानको श्रम र सम्बन्धसँग जोड्छन्, र अध्ययनले प्रस्तुतिलाई सांस्कृतिक ज्ञानका रूपमा लिन्छ: गति र संगीतले संसार बुझ्ने तरिका बोल्न सक्छन्, पाठको चित्रण मात्र होइन। यो अनुसन्धानकर्ताको विश्लेषण हो, हरेक कलाकारले दिने सार्वभौम व्याख्या होइन। नर्तक "कथा नाचिरहेको" भन्न सक्छिन्, वृद्ध "वर्ष नाचिरहेको" भन्न सक्छन्; दुवैले एउटै शृङ्खलालाई फरक छेउबाट वर्णन गरिरहेका हुन्छन्।

ज्ञान शब्दमा मात्र छैन। शरीर कसरी चल्छ भन्नेमा पनि छ, र त्यही लेख्न सबैभन्दा गाह्रो भाग हो।

सन्यासीको प्रसंग सबैभन्दा शान्त र धेरै दर्शकका लागि सबैभन्दा छुने प्रसंग हो: गाउँबाट टाढा भएको जीवन, गाउँकै सामु नाचिएको, र जसको काका वा हजुरबुबा त्यो बाटो गएका छन् भन्ने सबैले बुझ्छन्।

धानको प्रसंगमा दर्शकले आफ्नो वर्ष देख्छ, र यसैले घाटु राजा-रानीको कथा हुँदै पनि गाउँको कथा बन्छ। जसले बाली रोपेको छैन, उसले पनि बारी चिन्छ; जसले रोपेको छ, उसले आफ्नै हात नाचिएको देख्छ।

## [en] Music, movement and specialist knowledge

The movement itself is slow and deliberate: rotating turns, bending and rising, overlapping voices, and drum patterns that answer the dancers rather than holding a mechanically fixed beat. Nothing about it is fast, and the slowness is what allows a performance to last a night without exhausting the dancers or the audience.

The guru has a special role, initiating sections and explaining words that other performers may know only approximately. That detail matters for anyone tempted to treat the songs as a text. The performers may carry the melody and the movement with great precision while their understanding of the archaic vocabulary is partial, and the guru is the person who holds the meaning as well as the sequence — which is why the office is not casually filled.

Moisala could not identify the performance language simply as ordinary Gurung or Nepali, and other local accounts identify an older Nepali form. The difference is worth keeping, because it warns against publishing an invented standard translation based on how a performance looks. A translator who has not resolved which language is being sung cannot produce a version that is faithful to anything except his own expectation.

Which is why this chapter describes the performance's structure and refuses to supply lyrics it cannot source.

The movement is learned by watching and by being corrected, not from notation, which is why a tradition of this kind lives in bodies and in the patience of a guru rather than in a book that could be consulted when the last teacher dies.

A drum pattern that answers the dancers is a conversation, and it means the drummers must watch as closely as the dancers listen; a fixed beat would let everyone stop paying attention.

## [ne] संगीत, गति र विशेषज्ञ ज्ञान

गति आफैँ ढिलो र सोचविचारपूर्ण छ: घुम्ने फन्का, निहुरिने र उठ्ने, एकअर्कामा मिसिने आवाज, र नर्तकलाई जवाफ दिने मादलको ताल, मेसिनजस्तो स्थिर बिट होइन। यसमा केही पनि छिटो छैन, र त्यही ढिलोपनले रातभर प्रस्तुति चलाउन दिन्छ, नर्तक वा दर्शक नथाकी।

गुरुको विशेष भूमिका हुन्छ: खण्ड सुरु गर्ने र अरू कलाकारले ठ्याक्कै नजानेका शब्द बुझाउने। त्यो विवरणले गीतलाई पाठ मान्न हतारिनेलाई महत्त्वपूर्ण कुरा सिकाउँछ। कलाकारले धुन र गति ठ्याक्कै बोक्न सक्छन् जबकि पुरानो शब्दावलीको बुझाइ आंशिक हुन्छ, र गुरु नै त्यो मानिस हुन् जसको हातमा अर्थ र क्रम दुवै छ — त्यसैले यो पद हचुवामा भरिँदैन।

Moisala ले प्रस्तुतिको भाषा साधारण गुरुङ वा नेपाली भनेर चिन्न सकिनन्, र अरू स्थानीय विवरणले पुरानो नेपाली रूप भन्छन्। यो भेद राख्नु जरुरी छ, किनकि यसले प्रस्तुतिको देखावटका आधारमा काल्पनिक मानक अनुवाद छाप्नबाट रोक्छ। कुन भाषा गाइँदै छ भन्ने नटुङ्ग्याएको अनुवादकले आफ्नै अपेक्षाबाहेक कुनै कुराप्रति इमानदार हुन सक्दैन।

त्यसैले यो अध्याय प्रस्तुतिको ढाँचा वर्णन गर्छ र स्रोत नभएका गीतका शब्द दिन इन्कार गर्छ। मादलको ताल जवाफ हो, र त्यसको अर्थ मादलवालाले पनि नर्तकजत्तिकै ध्यान दिनुपर्छ भन्ने हो; स्थिर बिटले सबैलाई ध्यान दिन छाड्न दिन्थ्यो।

नर्तकलाई जवाफ दिने ताल कुराकानी हो, र त्यसको अर्थ मादलवालाले पनि नर्तकजत्तिकै ध्यान दिनुपर्छ भन्ने हो; स्थिर बिटले सबैलाई ध्यान दिन छाड्न दिन्थ्यो।

गुरु वाचनको अर्थ राख्ने मानिस हुन् भन्ने कुरा अभिलेखकर्ताका लागि चेतावनी हो: गीतको शब्द लिपिबद्ध गर्नेले पहिले त्यसको अर्थ सोध्नुपर्छ, र नपाए नपाएको भन्नुपर्छ।

## [en] Ritual meaning and respectful documentation

The sources also describe divine presence or possession in some performances, and heavy responsibilities for the guru and supporting participants. These are attributed beliefs and observations, not a tutorial for inducing a ritual state, and they should be read as reports of what participants say happens rather than as instructions.

The distinction between kinds of Ghatu matters here too. Festival presentations, selected episodes and full village observances are not interchangeable, and a description that blends them produces a tradition nobody practises. A permitted recording should name the performers, place, date and type of performance, because without those four details the recording cannot be used responsibly by anyone later.

The photograph on this page shows Ghatu performers at a Kathmandu Lhosar gathering; it is not evidence of the full annual ceremony described in the research. A stage in a city hall and a courtyard at Baisakh Purnima are two different events that share dancers, songs and a name.

The next chapter turns to a gathering that was less a performance than a way of growing up.

Consent for a recording belongs to the performers and the hosts together, and it can be withdrawn for reasons a visitor will not hear explained. A photograph taken at a festival is not a licence to describe the rite it accompanied.

Naming the type of performance is the difference between a record and a rumour, and it is the detail most often missing from the clips that circulate online.

## [ne] कर्मकाण्डी अर्थ र सम्मानजनक अभिलेख

स्रोतहरूले केही प्रस्तुतिमा दैवी उपस्थिति वा आवेशको वर्णन गर्छन्, र गुरु तथा सहयोगी सहभागीको गहिरो जिम्मेवारीको पनि। यी श्रेय दिइएका विश्वास र अवलोकन हुन्, कर्मकाण्डी अवस्था ल्याउने निर्देशिका होइनन्, र सहभागीले के हुन्छ भन्छन् भन्ने रिपोर्टका रूपमा पढ्नुपर्छ।

घाटुका किसिमको भेद पनि यहीँ महत्त्वपूर्ण हुन्छ। चाडपर्वको प्रस्तुति, छानिएका प्रसंग र गाउँको पूरा विधि एकअर्काका विकल्प होइनन्, र तिनलाई मिसाउने वर्णनले कसैले नगर्ने परम्परा बनाउँछ। अनुमति पाएको रेकर्डिङले कलाकार, ठाउँ, मिति र प्रस्तुतिको किसिम खुलाउनुपर्छ, किनकि ती चार विवरणबिना त्यो रेकर्डिङ पछि कसैले जिम्मेवारीसाथ प्रयोग गर्न सक्दैन।

यो पृष्ठको तस्बिर काठमाडौँको ल्होसार भेलामा घाटु कलाकार देखाउँछ; यो अनुसन्धानमा वर्णन गरिएको पूरा वार्षिक विधिको प्रमाण होइन। सहरको हलको मञ्च र बैशाख पूर्णिमाको आँगन एउटै कलाकार, गीत र नाम बाँड्ने दुई फरक घटना हुन्।

अर्को अध्याय त्यो भेलातिर जान्छ जो प्रस्तुतिभन्दा कम, र हुर्कने तरिकाभन्दा बढी थियो।

प्रस्तुतिको किसिम खुलाउनु रेकर्ड र हल्लाको फरक हो, र अनलाइन घुम्ने क्लिपमा प्रायः छुट्ने विवरण पनि त्यही हो।

अनुमति रेकर्डिङभन्दा अघि आउँछ। कलाकारले आफ्नो नाच देखाउन मन गरे पनि शब्द लेख्न नदिन सक्छन्, र त्यो अडान विरोधाभास होइन — देखाउने र हस्तान्तरण गर्ने दुई फरक कुरा हुन्।
