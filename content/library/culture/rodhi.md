## [en] An institution rather than a venue label

Rodhi is usually introduced as a place where young people gather, sing and form relationships, and that description is not wrong; it is simply too small. The older guide also connects Rodhi with practical cooperation, which changes what kind of institution it was.

The difference is between a venue and a group. A group and the relationships among its members matter as much as the physical house where people meet, and the house is only where the group happens to be. Calling a modern entertainment venue a Rodhi does not establish that it reproduces the village institution ethnographers described, any more than calling a building a school makes what happens inside it an education.

What that institution was takes some unpacking, because most of its structure was social rather than architectural. It had hosts, rules, obligations, visitors, a season and a reputation, and all of those are harder to photograph than a room with young people in it.

The first thing to understand is who was responsible for it.

The name has travelled further than the institution. A Rodhi in a Kathmandu or Pokhara street may be a restaurant with a stage, and a visitor who has eaten in one has learned something about the word and nothing about the village arrangement that gave it to the city.

The distinction matters most to the people who remember the institution, because they are usually the ones asked to explain why a restaurant with a folk band is not the thing their mothers hosted.

## [ne] स्थानको नामभन्दा माथिको संस्था

रोधीलाई प्रायः युवा भेला हुने, गाउने र सम्बन्ध बनाउने ठाउँ भनेर चिनाइन्छ, र त्यो वर्णन गलत होइन; त्यो मात्र सानो हो। पुरानो मार्गदर्शिकाले रोधीलाई व्यावहारिक सहकार्यसँग पनि जोड्छ, र त्यसले यो संस्था के थियो भन्ने कुरा फेर्छ।

भेद ठाउँ र समूहबीचको हो। समूह र त्यसका सदस्यबीचको सम्बन्ध मानिस भेट्ने भौतिक घरजत्तिकै महत्त्वपूर्ण हुन्छ, र घर त केवल त्यो ठाउँ हो जहाँ समूह पर्खाइमा बस्छ। आजको मनोरञ्जन-स्थललाई रोधी भन्नुले गाउँको त्यो संस्था पुनरुत्पादन गरेको स्थापित गर्दैन, जसरी भवनलाई विद्यालय भन्नुले भित्र हुने कामलाई शिक्षा बनाउँदैन।

त्यो संस्था के थियो भन्ने बुझ्न अलि खोल्नुपर्छ, किनकि यसको धेरै ढाँचा भवनभन्दा सामाजिक थियो। यसका आयोजक, नियम, दायित्व, पाहुना, ऋतु र प्रतिष्ठा थिए, र ती सबै युवा भएको कोठाको तस्बिरभन्दा लेख्न गाह्रो छन्।

पहिलो कुरा यो बुझ्नु हो: यसको जिम्मेवारी कसको थियो।

रोधी शब्द अहिले बढी चल्छ, तर त्यसको अर्थ साँघुरिएको छ। पुरानो बुझाइमा रोधी एउटा सम्बन्ध थियो — कसैको घरसँग, कसैको समूहसँग — र त्यो सम्बन्धका दायित्व थिए।

भेद सबैभन्दा बढी त्यसैलाई मतलब पर्छ जसले संस्था सम्झिन्छन्, किनकि उनीहरूलाई नै सोधिन्छ — लोकगीत बजाउने रेस्टुरेन्ट उनीहरूकी आमाले आयोजना गरेको कुरा किन होइन।

नाम नै सबैभन्दा ठूलो भ्रम हो: एउटा घर, एउटा समूह र एउटा साँझ — तीनैलाई रोधी भनिँदा कुन कुरा बाँच्यो भन्ने प्रश्नै हराउँछ।

## [en] Hosts and collective responsibility

Macfarlane and Indrabahadur Gurung describe groups meeting in the home of a member's parent or another adult who took a guiding role, called the mother or father of the Rodhi, with adults present during visiting and overnight gatherings. That arrangement is the institution's spine. The young people were not unsupervised; they were hosted, and the host's standing was involved in what happened under the roof.

This is a historically documented arrangement, not a universal specification for every Rodhi house, and it challenges the outsider's tendency to equate mixed sociability, song and courtship with an absence of responsibility. The equation is common because it is easy: young men and women together at night, singing, in a village — the imagination supplies the rest. The record supplies something more structured: a named adult, a house that could be visited, and behaviour that would be discussed afterwards with the host as much as with the young people.

Responsibility also ran the other way. A group that behaved badly in a host's house damaged that house's name, and a host who could not manage a group stopped being asked. Reputation worked as regulation where no police existed, and it worked because villages are small and memories are long.

From a single house, the institution reached outward.

Adult presence changed the meaning of courtship. A young man's visit was known to his hosts, discussed by them and remembered by the household, which made the relationship public in a way that a private meeting could never be.

A host's authority was moral rather than legal: it rested on being the kind of adult whose house young people could be sent to, and on being able to say afterwards what had happened there without embarrassment.

A modern reader may find the arrangement surprising in both directions: more freedom than a strict household would allow, and more supervision than a story about village romance usually admits.

## [ne] आयोजक र सामूहिक जिम्मेवारी

Macfarlane र Indrabahadur Gurung ले समूहहरू सदस्यको आमाबुबा वा मार्गदर्शक भूमिका लिने अर्को वयस्कको घरमा भेला हुने वर्णन गर्छन्, जसलाई रोधीको आमा वा बुबा भनिन्थ्यो, र भेटघाट तथा रातभर बसाइका बेला वयस्क पनि उपस्थित हुन्थे। त्यो व्यवस्था नै संस्थाको मेरुदण्ड हो। युवा बेवास्तामा थिएनन्; उनीहरू आयोजित थिए, र त्यो छानामुनि के हुन्छ भन्ने कुरामा आयोजकको प्रतिष्ठा जोडिएको थियो।

यो ऐतिहासिक रूपमा अभिलेख भएको व्यवस्था हो, हरेक रोधी घरको सार्वभौम ढाँचा होइन, र यसले बाहिरी मानिसको त्यो प्रवृत्तिलाई चुनौती दिन्छ जसले मिश्रित सामाजिकता, गीत र प्रणयलाई जिम्मेवारीको अभावसँग बराबर गर्छ। त्यो बराबरी सजिलो भएर सामान्य छ: युवक-युवती रातमा सँगै, गाउँमा, गाउँदै — कल्पनाले बाँकी कुरा आफैँ जोडिदिन्छ। अभिलेखले त्योभन्दा संरचित कुरा दिन्छ: नाम खुलेको वयस्क, भेट्न जान मिल्ने घर, र पछि आयोजकसँगै छलफल हुने व्यवहार।

जिम्मेवारी उल्टो पनि चल्थ्यो। आयोजकको घरमा नराम्रो व्यवहार गर्ने समूहले त्यो घरको नाम बिगार्थ्यो, र समूह धान्न नसक्ने आयोजकलाई फेरि बोलाइँदैन थियो। प्रतिष्ठा नै नियमन थियो, जहाँ प्रहरी थिएन, र त्यो चल्थ्यो किनकि गाउँ सानो छ र सम्झना लामो।

एक घरबाट संस्था बाहिरतिर फैलिन्थ्यो।

आयोजकको अधिकार नैतिक थियो, कानुनी होइन: त्यो यस्तो वयस्क हुनुमा टिकेको थियो जसको घरमा युवालाई पठाउन सकिन्थ्यो, र भोलि त्यहाँ के भयो भनेर लाज नमानी भन्न सकिन्थ्यो।

वयस्कको उपस्थितिले प्रणयको अर्थ फेर्थ्यो। युवकको भेट आयोजकलाई थाहा हुन्थ्यो, उनीहरूले चर्चा गर्थे र घरले सम्झिन्थ्यो, जसले सम्बन्धलाई निजी भेटले कहिल्यै नबनाउने सार्वजनिकता दिन्थ्यो।

आयोजक भन्ने पद नै एउटा जिम्मेवारी थियो: घर दिनु, भेलाको समय तोक्नु, र भोलि कसैले सोध्दा जवाफ दिन तयार हुनु। त्यसैले रोधी कुनै भवनको नाम होइन, कसैको जिम्मेवारीको नाम थियो।

## [en] Singing, visiting and a wider network

The guide also describes visits between groups in neighbouring villages, sometimes involving substantial walks at night and a return before daytime chores. That detail is worth pausing on, because it sets the scale: a Rodhi was not an evening's entertainment at the end of the lane but a network that could require hours of walking in the dark and still get everyone home for the morning's work.

Singing and joking created relationships across settlements as well as within a home group, which is why Rodhi should not be reduced to a stage show or one style of duet. The exchanges between groups were the mechanism by which young people from different villages knew each other at all — and in a society where marriage moves a woman to another village, that knowledge had consequences beyond a night's amusement.

Its social setting could involve age peers, hosts, visitors and work companions together, so the group was not a closed circle of friends. It was closer to an institution with a membership that changed, visitors who had to be received, and a calendar of occasions. A reader who imagines a village disco has the noise right and the structure wrong.

And the structure included work, which is where the modern division between leisure and labour breaks down.

A group that walked three hours to sing in another village was also a group that could be judged on the way, and the walk was part of the courtship: who came, who sang, who was sent home early and who was invited back.

The visiting also had an economic edge, since a village that received visitors from another settlement was also a village with marriages to arrange, and the two kinds of traffic used the same paths.

## [ne] गीत, भेटघाट र फराकिलो सञ्जाल

मार्गदर्शिकाले छिमेकी गाउँका समूहबीचको भेटघाट पनि वर्णन गर्छ, कहिलेकाहीँ रातमा लामो हिँडाइ र बिहानको कामअघि फर्कने सर्तसहित। त्यो विवरणमा रोकिनु जरुरी छ, किनकि यसले मापन तोक्छ: रोधी बाटोको छेउको साँझको मनोरञ्जन होइन, तर अन्धकारमा घण्टौँ हिँडाइ माग्न सक्ने र बिहानको कामका लागि सबैलाई घर पुर्‍याउन सक्ने सञ्जाल थियो।

गीत र ठट्टाले एउटै घरको समूहभित्रजत्तिकै बस्तीबीच पनि सम्बन्ध बनाउँथे, त्यसैले रोधीलाई मञ्चको कार्यक्रम वा एउटै किसिमको जुहारीमा खुम्च्याउनु हुँदैन। समूहबीचको आदानप्रदान नै त्यो कुरा थियो जसले फरक गाउँका युवाले एकअर्कालाई चिन्थे — र जहाँ विवाहले महिलालाई अर्को गाउँमा सार्छ, त्यहाँ त्यो चिनाइको परिणाम रातको रमाइलोभन्दा बढी हुन्थ्यो।

यसको सामाजिक परिवेशमा समउमेरका साथी, आयोजक, पाहुना र कामका सहयोगी सँगै हुन्थे, त्यसैले समूह साथीहरूको बन्द घेरा थिएन। यो सदस्यता फेरिने, पाहुना स्वागत गर्नुपर्ने र अवसरको पात्रो भएको संस्थाजस्तो थियो। गाउँको डिस्को कल्पना गर्नेले हल्ला ठीक पाउँछ, ढाँचा गलत।

ढाँचामा काम पनि थियो, र यहीँ आधुनिक फुर्सद-श्रमको भेद टुट्छ।

भेटघाटको आर्थिक पक्ष पनि थियो, किनकि अर्को बस्तीबाट पाहुना आउने गाउँमा विवाह मिलाउने काम पनि हुन्थ्यो, र दुवै किसिमको आवागमन एउटै बाटो प्रयोग गर्थ्यो।

तीन घण्टा हिँडेर अर्को गाउँमा गाउन जाने समूह बाटोमै पनि मूल्याङ्कन हुन्थ्यो, र हिँडाइ नै प्रणयको भाग थियो: को आयो, कसले गायो, कसलाई चाँडै घर पठाइयो र कसलाई फेरि बोलाइयो।

बाटो लामो हुँदा समूहले बीचमा विश्राम गर्थ्यो, र त्यो विश्राम पनि भेटघाटको भाग थियो — कसले के खायो, कसले के गाए, कसले कसलाई सम्झ्यो।

## [en] Work and leisure could overlap

In the same account young people cooperated in practical tasks, and evening sociability could accompany processing beans or wool. The boundary between a work party and a musical gathering was therefore less rigid than a modern timetable of work and entertainment suggests.

Think of what processing wool involves: several people, a long evening, hands busy and mouths free. Song is not an interruption of that work; it is what makes three hours of it bearable, and the group that sings together through the wool will sing together at a festival. The same is true of beans, of transplanting, of any task that needs many hands in one place at one time.

Harrison and Macfarlane separately document exchange and paid labour groups, which provides context without proving that every collective labour party was a Rodhi. The caution is necessary because the two institutions overlapped in personnel and in form while remaining distinguishable in purpose: one organised work, the other organised sociability, and a given evening might serve both without becoming either.

That overlap is also why Rodhi could be criticised and defended in the same breath — as idleness by one account and as cooperation by another. Both descriptions can be true of different evenings.

What happened to the institution is a local story before it is a general one.

Work songs are the clearest evidence of the overlap. A song that keeps a line of planters in step is doing something a timetable cannot, and the same song sung in a courtyard in the evening is doing something else with the same words.

Seasonality shaped it as well. There are months when nobody has an evening free, and months when the work is done by dark, and the institution's calendar followed the fields rather than the other way round.

That is also why the institution is hard to classify in the language of leisure. It was not work and not entertainment, but an evening in which the two were done at once by the same people for the same reasons.

## [ne] काम र फुर्सद ओभरल्याप

उही विवरणमा युवाले व्यावहारिक काममा सहकार्य गर्थे, र साँझको सामाजिकता सिमी वा ऊन प्रशोधनसँगै जान सक्थ्यो। त्यसैले कामको भेला र गीतको भेलाबीचको सीमा आजको काम-मनोरञ्जन तालिकाले सोचेभन्दा पातलो थियो।

ऊन प्रशोधनमा के लाग्छ सोच्नुहोस्: धेरै मानिस, लामो साँझ, हात व्यस्त र मुख फुक्का। गीत त्यो कामको अवरोध होइन; त्यो तीन घण्टा सह्य बनाउने कुरा हो, र ऊनसँगै गाएको समूह चाडमा पनि सँगै गाउँछ। सिमीमा, रोपाइँमा, एकै ठाउँमा धेरै हात चाहिने जुनसुकै काममा पनि त्यही लागू हुन्छ।

Harrison र Macfarlane ले विनिमय र ज्याला-श्रम समूह अलगै अभिलेख गर्छन्, जसले सन्दर्भ दिन्छ तर हरेक सामूहिक श्रम-भेला रोधी थियो भन्ने प्रमाण दिँदैन। होसियारी जरुरी छ किनकि दुई संस्था मानिस र रूपमा मिल्थे तर उद्देश्यमा छुट्टिन्थे: एउटाले काम संगठित गर्थ्यो, अर्कोले सामाजिकता, र कुनै साँझ दुवैको सेवा गर्न सक्थ्यो, कुनै एउटा नबनी।

त्यही ओभरल्यापका कारण रोधीको प्रशंसा र आलोचना एउटै सासमा हुन सक्थ्यो — कसैको हिसाबमा अल्छी, कसैको हिसाबमा सहकार्य। दुवै वर्णन फरक साँझका लागि सही हुन सक्छन्।

संस्थालाई के भयो भन्ने कुरा सामान्य कुराअघि स्थानीय कथा हो।

ऋतुले पनि आकार दिन्थ्यो। कुनै महिना कसैलाई साँझ फुर्सद हुँदैन, कुनै महिना अँध्यारोअघि नै काम सकिन्छ, र संस्थाको पात्रो खेतको पात्रो पछ्याउँथ्यो, उल्टो होइन।

कामका गीत नै ओभरल्यापको सबैभन्दा स्पष्ट प्रमाण हुन्। रोप्ने लहरलाई तालमा राख्ने गीतले त्यो गर्छ जो तालिकाले गर्न सक्दैन, र त्यही गीत साँझ आँगनमा गाइँदा उही शब्दले अर्कै काम गर्छ।

## [en] Change in Ghandruk

Change came to Rodhi as it came to the villages. Saroj Raj Panta's 2023 study examines Rodhi culture and the factors in its transformation specifically in Ghandruk, Kaski, offering a local setting for asking how migration, new livelihoods and changing social expectations affect the institution.

The older guide discusses criticism of Rodhi and the effect of young people leaving villages, and the two concerns are connected. A group whose members are in Pokhara, Kathmandu or the Gulf cannot meet in a host's house; a village with few young adults cannot sustain visits between settlements; and an institution that depended on adult hosts and overnight guests is hard to keep in a town where nobody has a courtyard.

Neither source proves that Rodhi has disappeared everywhere or that one cause explains change in every place. That restraint is not hedging for its own sake. A tradition can be flourishing in one valley and finished in another, and a reader who wants to know about a particular place needs the particular study, not a national verdict.

What the sources do show is a pattern familiar from the rest of this book: an institution that was embedded in work, housing and neighbourliness struggles when all three change at once, and adapts by becoming something more deliberate and less ordinary.

Which raises the question of how it should be remembered.

Local studies of this kind are worth more than any general survey, because they can say which year, which ward and which families, and because a reader from Ghandruk can check them against what her grandmother says.

What the Ghandruk study can do that a general essay cannot is show sequence: which change came first, which households were affected, and what the institution looked like in the decade between.

A study of one ward in one district is also a corrective to the assumption that a tradition changes everywhere at the same speed, or for the same reason, or in the same decade.

## [ne] घान्द्रुकमा परिवर्तन

परिवर्तन गाउँमा आएजसरी रोधीमा पनि आयो। Saroj Raj Panta को २०२३ को अध्ययनले रोधी संस्कृति र त्यसको रूपान्तरणका कारक विशेषगरी घान्द्रुक, कास्कीमा हेर्छ, र बसाइँसराइ, नयाँ आजीविका तथा बदलिँदो सामाजिक अपेक्षाले संस्थालाई कसरी छोयो भन्ने प्रश्नको स्थानीय सेटिङ दिन्छ।

पुरानो मार्गदर्शिकाले रोधीको आलोचना र युवा गाउँ छाड्ने असरको चर्चा गर्छ, र दुई चिन्ता जोडिएका छन्। जसका सदस्य पोखरा, काठमाडौँ वा खाडीमा छन्, त्यो समूह आयोजकको घरमा भेला हुन सक्दैन; जहाँ थोरै युवा छन्, त्यहाँ बस्तीबीचको भेटघाट टिक्दैन; र वयस्क आयोजक र रातभरका पाहुनामा टिकेको संस्था त्यहाँ कठिन हुन्छ जहाँ कसैको आँगन छैन।

कुनै पनि स्रोतले रोधी सबैतिर हरायो वा एउटै कारणले सबै ठाउँमा परिवर्तन भयो भन्ने प्रमाण दिँदैन। त्यो संयम निष्क्रियता होइन। परम्परा एउटा उपत्यकामा फस्टाइरहेको र अर्कोमा सकिएको हुन सक्छ, र कुनै ठाउँबारे जान्न चाहनेलाई त्यही स्थानीय अध्ययन चाहिन्छ, राष्ट्रिय फैसला होइन।

स्रोतले देखाउने ढाँचा यही पुस्तकका अरू अध्यायमा परिचित छ: काम, आवास र छिमेकसँग बाँधिएको संस्था तीनै एकैचोटि बदलिँदा सङ्घर्ष गर्छ, र अझ जानाजान र अझ कम साधारण बनेर मिलाउँछ।

यसले यो प्रश्न उठाउँछ: यसलाई कसरी सम्झिने।

घान्द्रुकको अध्ययनले सामान्य निबन्धले नसक्ने काम गर्छ: क्रम देखाउँछ — कुन परिवर्तन पहिले आयो, कुन घर प्रभावित भए, र बीचको दशकमा संस्था कस्तो देखिन्थ्यो।

यस किसिमका स्थानीय अध्ययन कुनै सामान्य सर्वेक्षणभन्दा मूल्यवान् हुन्छन्, किनकि तिनले कुन वर्ष, कुन वडा र कुन परिवार भन्न सक्छन्, र घान्द्रुककी पाठकले हजुरआमाको भनाइसँग भिडाएर जाँच्न सक्छिन्।

रूपान्तरणको क्रम बुझ्न गाह्रो हुनुको कारण यो हो कि संस्था एकैचोटि बन्द भएन; पहिले भेला कम हुँदै गयो, पछि आयोजक पाउन गाह्रो भयो, र अन्तिममा नाम मात्र बाँकी रह्यो।

## [en] Remembering without romanticising

A fuller account would include former participants, current organisers, women, young people and local hosts, with the place and period named. That is not a formality about methods. Rodhi is remembered differently depending on who is speaking: a woman may remember friendships and songs, a parent may remember anxiety, a host may remember the work of feeding twenty people, and a young person in Kathmandu may remember a video rather than a courtyard.

People may value different aspects — song, friendship, work cooperation or a sense of village belonging — and preserving the institution's history does not require treating every older arrangement as ideal or every newer form as inauthentic. Romanticising the past and dismissing it are the same error in different directions: both replace the evidence with a mood.

No song lyrics, recordings or private accounts of courtship are invented here. What can be said is what the sources say, attributed to the places and people who said it, and left open where the record is thin.

What survives of that social world is the subject of the next part of the book.

Oral history is the only method that reaches most of this, and it needs time: several sittings, several families, and a willingness to write down disagreement instead of averaging it into a single memory.

The people best placed to describe Rodhi are often the least likely to be asked, because they are women who married into a village and remember both their own group and the one they joined.

Writing about it well means resisting both the village idyll and the account in which everything old was oppressive, since the same institution could be a young woman's friendship network and a household's labour arrangement at the same time.

## [ne] रोमान्टिक नबनाई सम्झना

पूरा विवरणमा पूर्व सहभागी, वर्तमान आयोजक, महिला, युवा र स्थानीय आयोजक पर्नुपर्छ, ठाउँ र अवधि खुलेको हुनुपर्छ। यो विधिगत औपचारिकता होइन। रोधी को बोल्दै छ भन्ने आधारमा फरक सम्झिन्छ: महिलाले साथी र गीत सम्झिन सक्छिन्, आमाबुबाले चिन्ता, आयोजकले बीस जनालाई खुवाउनुको श्रम, र काठमाडौँको युवाले आँगन होइन भिडियो सम्झिन सक्छ।

मानिसले फरक पक्षलाई मूल्य दिन सक्छन् — गीत, मित्रता, कामको सहकार्य वा गाउँसँगको अपनत्व — र संस्थाको इतिहास जोगाउन हरेक पुरानो व्यवस्थालाई आदर्श वा हरेक नयाँ रूपलाई नक्कली मान्नु पर्दैन। भूतलाई रोमान्टिक बनाउनु र त्यसलाई इन्कार गर्नु एउटै गल्तीका दुई दिशा हुन्: दुवैले प्रमाणको सट्टा भावना राख्छन्।

यहाँ कुनै गीतको शब्द, रेकर्डिङ वा प्रणयका निजी विवरण बनाइएको छैन। भन्न सकिने कुरा स्रोतले भनेको कुरा हो, जसको ठाउँ र मानिस जोडिएको छ, र जहाँ अभिलेख पातलो छ त्यहाँ खुला छाडिएको छ।

त्यो सामाजिक संसारबाट के बाँच्यो भन्ने कुरा पुस्तकको अर्को भागको विषय हो।

रोधीको वर्णन गर्न सबैभन्दा सक्षम मानिसलाई प्रायः सोधिँदैन, किनकि ती ती महिला हुन् जो विवाह गरेर गाउँ पसेका हुन् र आफ्नो समूह र पसेको समूह दुवै सम्झिन्छन्।

मौखिक इतिहास नै यसको धेरै भागमा पुग्ने एक मात्र विधि हो, र त्यसलाई समय चाहिन्छ: धेरै बैठक, धेरै परिवार, र असहमति एउटै सम्झनामा मिलाउनुभन्दा लेख्ने तयारी।

लेख्नेले आफ्नो पूर्वाग्रह जाँच्नुपर्ने ठाउँ पनि यही हो: "रोधी राम्रो थियो" वा "रोधी बिग्रियो" भन्नुभन्दा पहिले कसलाई सोधियो भन्ने खुलाउनुपर्छ।
