## [en] The war in Malaya

In December 1941 Japanese forces attacked Malaya and within weeks were fighting down the peninsula towards Singapore. Gurkha battalions were part of the British defence, fighting delaying actions at Kampar and elsewhere, and many were killed or captured when Singapore fell in February 1942. The survivors endured years of imprisonment or escape, and the campaign became one of the regiment's founding memories: a defeat that produced legends of discipline and endurance rather than triumph.

## [en] The Burma campaign

Burma was the longest and most brutal theatre for the Gurkhas. In 1942 they retreated with the British army from Rangoon to India, a march through jungle and monsoon that killed thousands. From 1944 they fought back: at Imphal and Kohima, where the Japanese advance into India was stopped, and then in the reconquest of Burma in 1944–45, through Meiktila and down to Rangoon. Gurung soldiers served throughout, and it was in Burma in May 1945 that Lachhiman Gurung of the 4th Gurkha Rifles won his Victoria Cross at Taungdaw, holding a section position through the night after being wounded and losing the use of his right hand. The jungle war produced a particular kind of veteran: young, decorated, often wounded, and familiar with a world far beyond the hills.

## [ne] बर्मा अभियान

गोर्खाका लागि बर्मा सबैभन्दा लामो र क्रूर मोर्चा थियो। १९४२ मा उनीहरू बेलायती सेनासँग रन्गुनबाट भारततिर पछि हटे, जङ्गल र मनसुन हुँदैको त्यो यात्राले हजारौँको ज्यान लियो। १९४४ देखि उनीहरूले प्रतिआक्रमण गरे: इम्फाल र कोहिमामा, जहाँ भारततिर जापानी अग्रगमन रोकियो, र त्यसपछि १९४४–४५ को बर्मा पुनर्विजयमा, मेइक्टिला हुँदै रन्गुनसम्म। गुरुङ सिपाहीले सबैतिर सेवा गरे, र मे १९४५ मा बर्मामै ४थ गोर्खा राइफल्सका Lachhiman Gurung (लछिमन गुरुङ) ले ताउङदाउमा भिक्टोरिया क्रस जिते — घाइते भएर दाहिने हातको प्रयोग गुमाएपछि पनि रातभर आफ्नो मोर्चा होल्ड गर्दै। जङ्गल-युद्धले विशेष किसिमको भूतपूर्व सिपाही बनायो: युवा, पदकधारी, प्रायः घाइते, र पहाडभन्दा धेरै टाढाको संसार चिनेको।

## [en] The Malayan Emergency and the garrisons

After the war, Gurkha battalions stayed in Malaya to fight the communist insurgency during the Emergency of 1948–60. They patrolled the jungle for years, working with local forces and police, and their success owed much to small-unit discipline and to the trust they built with rural communities. The brigade's headquarters moved to Malaya, and later to Hong Kong, where Gurkha battalions were stationed until the handover in 1997. The garrisons became small Nepali worlds: families lived in married quarters, children went to schools on camp, and shops sold Nepali food and religious items. Many soldiers who retired from these garrisons settled in Malaysia, Singapore or Hong Kong rather than returning to Nepal.

## [ne] मलाया इमर्जेन्सी र छाउनी

युद्धपछि गोर्खा बटालियन १९४८–६० को इमर्जेन्सीमा साम्यवादी विद्रोहविरुद्ध लड्न मलायामा रहे। तिनले वर्षौँ जङ्गलमा गस्ती गरे, स्थानीय सेना र प्रहरीसँग काम गरे, र सफलता धेरै हदसम्म सानो एकाइको अनुशासन र ग्रामीण समुदायसँग बनाएको विश्वासमा टेक्यो। ब्रिगेडको मुख्यालय मलाया र पछि हङकङ सर्‍यो, जहाँ १९९७ को हस्तान्तरणसम्म गोर्खा बटालियन खटिए। छाउनी साना नेपाली संसार बने: परिवार वैवाहिक आवासमा बस्थे, बालबालिका शिविरकै विद्यालयमा पढ्थे, र पसलमा नेपाली खाना तथा धार्मिक सामान बिक्थ्यो। यी छाउनीबाट अवकाश पाएका धेरै सिपाही नेपाल नफर्की मलेसिया, सिङ्गापुर वा हङकङमै बसे।

## [en] Life between two countries

Garrison life produced a culture that was neither Nepali nor British. Families spoke Nepali at home, English at school and Malay or Cantonese in the market; they celebrated Lhosar and Christmas; they sent money to Nepal and brought relatives out for visits. Children born in the camps often had no direct experience of the village their parents came from, and the stories they heard were filtered through the regiment. When the Hong Kong garrison closed in 1997, some families moved to Britain, some to Nepal, and some stayed in Asia, and the dispersal created the scattered communities that now organise themselves through associations and Lhosar events.

## [ne] दुई देशबीचको जीवन

छाउनी-जीवनले नेपाली पनि नभएको, बेलायती पनि नभएको संस्कृति बनायो। परिवार घरमा नेपाली, विद्यालयमा अङ्ग्रेजी र बजारमा मलाय वा क्यान्टोनिज बोल्थे; ल्होसार र क्रिसमस दुवै मनाउँथे; नेपाल पैसा पठाउँथे र नातेदारलाई भेट्न बोलाउँथे। शिविरमा जन्मेका बालबालिकालाई आफ्ना आमाबुबा आएको गाउँको प्रत्यक्ष अनुभव प्रायः हुँदैनथ्यो, र सुनेका कथा रेजिमेन्टबाट छानिएर आउँथे। १९९७ मा हङकङ छाउनी बन्द हुँदा केही परिवार बेलायत, केही नेपाल र केही एसियामै बसे, र त्यो छरिएर अहिले संस्था तथा ल्होसार कार्यक्रममार्फत आफूलाई संगठित गर्ने समुदाय बने।

## [en] Memory and the archive

The eastern garrisons are remembered through regimental histories, photographs, medals and family stories, but they are under-documented as social history. There are few interviews with the wives who ran the households, the children who grew up between languages, or the shopkeepers who served the camps. Veterans' associations and community archives have begun to collect material, and the closure of the Hong Kong garrison in 1997 prompted documentation projects in Britain and Nepal. For the Gurung community, this is the history that explains why a family in Aldershot, a family in Pokhara and a family in Hong Kong can share a surname and a set of stories but not a village.

## [ne] सम्झना र अभिलेख

पूर्वी छाउनी रेजिमेन्टल इतिहास, फोटो, पदक र पारिवारिक कथाबाट सम्झिन्छन्, तर सामाजिक इतिहासका रूपमा तिनको अभिलेख कम छ। घर चलाउने श्रीमती, दुई भाषाबीच हुर्केका बालबालिका वा शिविरमा पसल चलाउनेसँग अन्तर्वार्ता थोरै छन्। भूतपूर्व सिपाही संस्था र सामुदायिक अभिलेखले सामग्री संकलन सुरु गरेका छन्, र हङकङ छाउनी बन्द हुनुले केही अभिलेख परियोजना जगायो। गुरुङ समुदायका लागि यो त्यो इतिहास हो जसले एल्डरशटको परिवार, पोखराको परिवार र हङकङको परिवार एउटै थर र कथाका समूह बाँड्न सक्छन् तर गाउँ बाँड्दैनन् भन्ने व्याख्या गर्छ।
