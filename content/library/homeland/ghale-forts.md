## [en] What a kot was

In the hills, a kot was a fortified place on a ridge: dry-stone walls, a gate, a water source, room for a garrison and grain, and a view of the routes below. It was not usually a palace. The ruler's residence might be inside, but the kot's primary purpose was military and administrative — to hold a pass, to shelter people and animals in a raid, to store tax grain, and to signal to the next ridge with fire or smoke. A network of kots turned a valley into a territory that could be defended and taxed.

## [en] The named forts

Kaskikot, above the Mardi and Seti valleys, was the centre of the Kaski kingdom and is the best-known Ghale-associated fort. Ligligkot, in Gorkha district, is remembered in the foundation stories of the Shah dynasty, where a race to the fort is said to have decided who would rule. Gorkha's own hilltop fort became the seat of the expanding state. Nuwakot, on the trade route north of the valley, was fortified and fought over repeatedly. Each has its own traditions, and each has been studied far less than its historical importance warrants.

## [ne] नाम भएका किल्ला

मर्दी र सेती उपत्यकामाथिको कास्कीकोट कास्की राज्यको केन्द्र थियो र घलेसँग जोडिएको सबैभन्दा परिचित किल्ला हो। गोर्खा जिल्लाको लिगलिगकोट शाह वंशका स्थापना-कथामा सम्झिन्छ, जहाँ किल्लासम्मको दौडले कसले शासन गर्ने तय गरेको भनिन्छ। गोर्खाको आफ्नै डाँडाको किल्ला विस्तारशील राज्यको केन्द्र बन्यो। उपत्यकाको उत्तरमा व्यापार-मार्गमा पर्ने नुवाकोट किल्ला गरियो र बारम्बार लडाइँ भयो। हरेकका आफ्नै परम्परा छन्, र हरेक ऐतिहासिक महत्त्वले योग्य ठान्नेभन्दा धेरै कम अध्ययन भएका छन्।

## [en] How a fort worked

A fort was a small economy. It needed water, which meant cisterns or a spring inside the walls; food, which meant grain stores and a road to the fields below; fuel and fodder; and people to maintain the walls, watch the approaches and repair the gate. Garrisons were raised from the surrounding households, which is one way villages were tied to a ruler. In wartime, the fort sheltered the population and their animals; in peacetime, it collected tolls, stored tax grain and served as a court. A fort without a reliable water supply could not hold out, which is why the best sites are remembered as much for their springs as their walls.

## [ne] किल्ला कसरी चल्थ्यो

किल्ला सानो अर्थतन्त्र थियो। पानी चाहिन्थ्यो, त्यसैले पर्खालभित्र इनार वा मूल; खाना चाहिन्थ्यो, त्यसैले अन्न-भण्डार र तलका खेतसम्मको बाटो; इन्धन र घाँस; र पर्खाल मर्मत गर्ने, बाटो हेर्ने र ढोका बनाउने मानिस। सिपाही वरपरका घरबाट उठाइन्थ्यो, जुन गाउँलाई शासकसँग जोड्ने तरिकामध्ये एक थियो। युद्धमा किल्लाले जनता र पशु जोगाउँथ्यो; शान्तिमा दस्तुर उठाउँथ्यो, करको अन्न राख्थ्यो र अदालतका रूपमा काम गर्थ्यो। भरपर्दो पानीबिना किल्ला टिक्न सक्दैनथ्यो, त्यसैले उत्तम स्थल पर्खालजत्तिकै मूलका लागि पनि सम्झिन्छन्।

## [en] The later forts

The fort tradition did not end with the hill kingdoms. During the Anglo-Nepal war of 1814–16, Nepali forces defended improvised fortifications at Nalapani, Jaithak, Malaun and Devthal, and the British built their own posts to besiege them. In the nineteenth and twentieth centuries, the state built barracks, prisons and administrative offices rather than hill forts, and the old kots were abandoned, quarried for stone or covered by vegetation. Some are now picnic spots and viewpoint trails; others are barely visible.

## [ne] पछिका किल्ला

किल्लाको परम्परा पहाडी राज्यसँगै सकिएन। १८१४–१६ को अङ्ग्रेज-नेपाल युद्धमा नेपाली सेनाले नलापानी, जैथक, मलाउन र देउथलमा हतारका किल्ला रक्षा गरे, र बेलायतले घेराबन्दीका लागि आफ्नै चौकी बनाए। उन्नाइसौँ र बीसौँ शताब्दीमा राज्यले पहाडी किल्ला होइन, छाउनी, जेल र प्रशासनिक कार्यालय बनायो, र पुराना कोट परित्यक्त, ढुङ्गाका लागि खनेर वा वनस्पतिले ढाकिए। केही अहिले पिकनिक-स्थल र दृश्य-बाटो बनेका छन्; अरू मुस्किलले देखिन्छन्।

## [en] Tika seats and the ritual map

A fort held a territory; a head house held a clan, and the two geographies met at the tika. Bista records that the Ghale pads gather at the chiva-ti, the head house, on the tika day of Dashain, when each household arrives with the head of a ram it has slaughtered, and that a pad which quarrelled could set up its own head house and its own head field. Mani Raj Gurung's history of Gurung political change makes the same connection from the other direction: chiefly authority in the hills was not only military. The ruler who held a kot also patronised the shrines and festivals that made the territory a community, and the families who served him held ritual duties that outlasted his dynasty. That is why the important site for a clan is often not a ruin on a ridge but a house, a field or a spring that the family still visits.

The seats remembered in the traditions are therefore of two kinds. Kaskikot and Ligligkot are forts in the ordinary sense, with walls, gates and a view of the routes; the chiva-ti of a pad may be an ordinary house with a lineage shrine, a hearth and a stone that marks the head field. Both are places where authority was reproduced, and both are poorly documented. Local accounts can name the house and the family that keeps it; the written record can date a grant or a temple; neither alone can show how the two were connected before the nineteenth century. A history of Ghale rule that mentions only the forts would describe the defences and miss the institution.

## [ne] टिकाका आसन र कर्मकाण्डी नक्सा

किल्लाले भूभाग राख्थ्यो; मुख्य घरले क्ले राख्थ्यो, र दुई भूगोल टिकामा भेटिन्थे। बिष्ट लेख्छन्, घले पड दशैँको टिकाका दिन _चिभा-ति_, मुख्य घरमा भेला हुन्छन्, जब हरेक घरले काटेको बोकोको टाउको लिएर आउँछ, र झगडा परेको पडले आफ्नै मुख्य घर र आफ्नै मुख्य खेत बनाउन सक्थ्यो। मणिराज गुरुङको गुरुङ राजनीतिक परिवर्तनको इतिहासले त्यही सम्बन्ध अर्को दिशाबाट जोड्छ: पहाडमा मुखियापन केवल सैन्य थिएन। कोट राख्ने शासकले भूभागलाई समुदाय बनाउने देवालय र चाडको पनि संरक्षण गर्थ्यो, र उसको सेवा गर्ने परिवारसँग उसको राजवंशभन्दा लामो कर्मकाण्डी दायित्व हुन्थ्यो। त्यसैले क्लेका लागि महत्त्वपूर्ण स्थल प्रायः डाँडाको भग्नावशेष होइन, परिवारले अझै भेट्ने घर, खेत वा मूल हुन्छ।

परम्परामा सम्झिने आसन त्यसैले दुई किसिमका छन्। कास्कीकोट र लिगलिगकोट साधारण अर्थमा किल्ला हुन् — पर्खाल, ढोका र बाटो देखिने दृश्यसहित; पडको चिभा-ति साधारण घर हुन सक्छ, जहाँ वंशको देवालय, चुलो र मुख्य खेत चिनाउने ढुङ्गा हुन्छ। दुवै त्यही ठाउँ हुन् जहाँ अधिकार पुनरुत्पादन हुन्थ्यो, र दुवैको कागजात कमजोर छ। स्थानीय विवरणले घर र त्यसलाई राख्ने परिवारको नाम दिन सक्छ; लिखित अभिलेखले दान वा मन्दिरको मिति दिन सक्छ; कुनैले पनि उन्नाइसौँ शताब्दीअघि दुई कसरी जोडिएका थिए देखाउन सक्दैन। घले शासनको इतिहासमा किल्लाको मात्र उल्लेख गर्ने हो भने रक्षा-व्यवस्था वर्णन हुन्छ, संस्था छुट्छ।

## [en] Why excavation matters

Almost none of the major kots has been systematically excavated. Surface surveys have recorded walls, gateways, cisterns and potsherds, but without excavation there are no dates, no sequences of occupation and no evidence of what people ate, made or traded inside. That absence is why the history of hill states is still written largely from genealogies and land grants, which are the documents of rulers, not of the people who built and held the forts. A modest excavation programme at one or two sites would transform the field, and it would also give the communities around them a firmer basis for conservation.
