## [en] In Nepal

The 2021 National Population and Housing Census counted 543,790 people who identified as Gurung. That number is the beginning of every count in this chapter, and it is worth pausing on what it actually is: a count of people inside Nepal, on a particular night, who chose one name from a list.

Within those limits it is solid, and it is the figure to quote when a figure is needed. It is also the reason the rest of this chapter has to be careful, because a census counts categories and a community is not a category. The largest communities live in Gandaki Province, in Kaski, Lamjung, Syangja, Tanahun, Gorkha, Parbat and Manang, as well as in Kathmandu and Pokhara. Those districts are where the book's older chapters take place, and the census shows that the old homeland still holds the largest part of the population.

That last point is worth holding on to, because the story told about hill communities is usually a story of emptying villages. The census says something more complicated: the homeland still holds most Gurungs, even though it no longer holds most of their children's ambitions.

The distribution is not accidental. Kaski and Lamjung are the districts most closely associated with Gurkha recruitment and with the older settlement of the region; Gorkha and Tanahun lie along routes that carried soldiers, traders and labourers; Manang and Parbat are smaller, high and steep. A map of where Gurungs live is therefore also a map of where they could make a living — and, for a century and a half, of where a recruiting officer was willing to walk.

The figure also carries a warning about growth and decline that no single census can settle. A community that was counted at one number in 2021 was counted at a different number in 2011, and the difference reflects births, deaths, migration and, just as importantly, changes in how people answered the question. Census totals move for reasons that have nothing to do with demography, and anyone comparing decades should ask which of those reasons was at work.

## [ne] नेपालमा

सन् २०२१ को राष्ट्रिय जनगणना तथा आवास सर्वेक्षणले ५,४३,७९० जनाले आफूलाई गुरुङ भनेको देखायो। त्यो सङ्ख्या यो अध्यायका हरेक गन्तीको सुरुवात हो, र यो के हो भन्नेमा अलि रोकिनु जरुरी छ: नेपालभित्रका, एक तोकिएको रात, सूचीबाट एउटा नाम छान्ने मानिसको गन्ती।

ती सीमाभित्र त्यो ठोस छ, र सङ्ख्या चाहिँदा उद्धृत गर्नुपर्ने अङ्क त्यही हो। त्यही कारण यो अध्यायको बाँकी भाग होसियार हुनुपर्छ, किनकि जनगणना कोटि गन्छ र समुदाय कोटि होइन। सबैभन्दा ठूलो जनसङ्ख्या गण्डकी प्रदेशका कास्की, लमजुङ, स्याङ्जा, तनहुँ, गोर्खा, पर्वत र मनाङ जिल्लामा, साथै काठमाडौँ र पोखरामा छ। ती जिल्ला नै यो पुस्तकका पुराना अध्यायका ठाउँ हुन्, र जनगणनाले देखाउँछ कि पुरानो मातृभूमिले अझै जनसङ्ख्याको सबैभन्दा ठूलो भाग राखेको छ।

त्यो अन्तिम कुरा समातेर राख्नु जरुरी छ, किनकि पहाडी समुदायबारे सुनाइने कथा प्रायः गाउँ खाली हुने कथा हो। जनगणना अझ जटिल कुरा भन्छ: मातृभूमिले अझै धेरै गुरुङ राख्छ, भले उसले उनीहरूका छोराछोरीको महत्त्वाकांक्षा राख्न छाडेको छ।

वितरण संयोगले भएको होइन। कास्की र लमजुङ गोर्खा भर्ती र क्षेत्रको पुरानो बसोबाससँग सबैभन्दा नजिक जोडिएका जिल्ला हुन्; गोर्खा र तनहुँ ती बाटोमा छन् जसले सिपाही, व्यापारी र श्रमिक बोके; मनाङ र पर्वत साना, अग्ला र भिराला छन्। त्यसैले गुरुङ कहाँ बस्छन् भन्ने नक्सा उनीहरूले कहाँ जीविका चलाउन सकेको नक्सा पनि हो — र डेढ शताब्दीसम्म, भर्ती अधिकारी कहाँसम्म हिँड्न तयार थियो भन्ने पनि।

यो अङ्कले वृद्धि र ह्रासबारे एउटा चेतावनी पनि बोक्छ जो कुनै एउटा जनगणनाले टुङ्ग्याउन सक्दैन। सन् २०२१ मा एउटा सङ्ख्यामा गनिएको समुदाय सन् २०११ मा अर्को सङ्ख्यामा गनिएको थियो, र भेदले जन्म, मृत्यु, बसाइँसराइ र — त्यत्तिकै महत्त्वपूर्ण — मानिसले प्रश्नको जवाफ कसरी दिए भन्ने देखाउँछ।

## [en] Language and religion

Fewer people speak Gurung as a mother tongue than identify as Gurung, which is the pattern across Nepal's indigenous languages as Nepali spreads through schooling, markets and marriage.

The gap between the two numbers is the clearest measure of what is happening to the language, and it is wider among the young and the urban. A household can be entirely Gurung by name and entirely Nepali by speech, and in many towns that is now the ordinary case rather than the exception. The census records the outcome without explaining it, and the explanation is not mysterious: a language that is not used in school, in the market or on television has to be deliberately maintained, and deliberate maintenance is hard work.

Religion is recorded in the same census, and the picture is mixed in a way that matters. Many Gurungs reported Buddhism, while others reported Hinduism or Bon, and in practice many families mix traditions rather than choosing one: a Buddhist household may keep a shamanic rite, and a Hindu one may call a lama. The categories on the form are not the categories people live by, and a chapter that treated them as a description of belief would be wrong about most households in the hills.

The census question about language is more delicate than it looks. It asks for a mother tongue, which is supposed to be the language learned in childhood and still understood, and people answer it with different ideas in mind: the language their parents spoke, the language they speak best, or the language they would like to be counted under. For a community in which many adults are fluent in Nepali and understand Tamu Kyi without speaking it, the answer is a judgement as much as a fact, and it makes the language figures softer than the population figures.

None of this makes the language numbers meaningless; it makes them indicative. They show a direction clearly enough — fewer speakers, especially young ones — and the direction is confirmed by everything the schools, the organisations and the families report. What they cannot do is tell you how many people could hold a conversation in Tamu Kyi if they had to, which is the number that matters most for the language's future and the one no census collects.

## [ne] भाषा र धर्म

गुरुङ पहिचान गर्नेभन्दा गुरुङ भाषा मातृभाषाका रूपमा बोल्ने मानिस कम छन्, जो नेपालका आदिवासी भाषामा देखिने सामान्य ढाँचा हो — विद्यालय, बजार र विवाहहुँदै नेपाली फैलिँदै जाँदा।

दुई सङ्ख्याबीचको दूरी भाषालाई के भइरहेको छ भन्ने सबैभन्दा स्पष्ट मापन हो, र त्यो युवा तथा सहरीबीच अझ फराकिलो छ। एउटा घर नामले पूरै गुरुङ र बोलीले पूरै नेपाली हुन सक्छ, र धेरै सहरमा त्यो अब अपवाद होइन, सामान्य अवस्था हो। जनगणनाले नतिजा अभिलेख गर्छ, व्याख्या गर्दैन, र व्याख्या रहस्यमय छैन: विद्यालय, बजार वा टेलिभिजनमा नचल्ने भाषा जानाजान जोगाउनुपर्छ, र जानाजान जोगाउनु गाह्रो काम हो।

धर्म पनि उही जनगणनामा अभिलेख हुन्छ, र चित्र मिश्रित छ, जुन महत्त्वपूर्ण छ। धेरै गुरुङले बौद्ध धर्म बताए, अरूले हिन्दु वा बोन, र व्यवहारमा धेरै परिवारले एउटा छान्नुभन्दा परम्परा मिसाउँछन्: बौद्ध घरले पनि कर्मकाण्डी विधि राख्न सक्छ, र हिन्दु घरले पनि लामा बोलाउन सक्छ। फारमका कोटि मानिसले बाँच्ने कोटि होइनन्, र तिनलाई विश्वासको वर्णन मान्ने अध्याय पहाडका धेरै घरबारे गलत हुन्थ्यो।

भाषाबारेको जनगणना-प्रश्न देखिनुभन्दा नाजुक छ। यसले मातृभाषा सोध्छ, जो बाल्यकालमा सिकेको र अझै बुझिने भाषा मानिन्छ, र मानिसले फरक धारणा राखेर जवाफ दिन्छन्: आमाबुबाले बोलेको भाषा, आफूले राम्ररी बोल्ने भाषा, वा जस्मुनि गनिन चाहेको भाषा। जहाँ धेरै वयस्क नेपालीमा धाराप्रवाह छन् र तामु क्यि बोल्न नसके पनि बुझ्छन्, त्यहाँ जवाफ तथ्यजत्तिकै निर्णय पनि हो, र त्यसले भाषाका अङ्कलाई जनसङ्ख्याका अङ्कभन्दा नरम बनाउँछ।

यसले भाषाका अङ्कलाई अर्थहीन बनाउँदैन; सङ्केतात्मक बनाउँछ। तिनले दिशा पर्याप्त स्पष्ट देखाउँछन् — कम बोल्ने, विशेषगरी कम युवा — र दिशा विद्यालय, संस्था र परिवारले भनेको कुराले पुष्टि गर्छ। तर तिनले भन्न सक्दैनन् कति मानिसले आवश्यक पर्दा तामु क्यिमा कुराकानी गर्न सक्छन्, जो भाषाको भविष्यका लागि सबैभन्दा महत्त्वपूर्ण सङ्ख्या हो र कुनै जनगणनाले उठाउँदैन।

## [en] Beyond Nepal

Outside Nepal the counting becomes guesswork. Large communities live in India, especially Sikkim, Darjeeling and the north-east, and in the United Kingdom, Hong Kong, the United States and other countries, but the figures come from different sources and different years and may count people in different ways.

Some count citizenship, some count language, some count membership of an association. A figure compiled from community estimates may count everyone who has ever attended a Lhosar; a figure from a national census may count only those who wrote the name on the form. Neither is wrong; they are answers to different questions, and the mistake is to put them in the same table and add them up.

The consequence is that any global number for Gurungs is an estimate wearing the clothes of a statistic. A responsible account says "tens of thousands" and names the survey it came from. An irresponsible one says "one million" and names nothing, and then gets repeated until it looks like a fact. This chapter uses numbers where they are measured and refuses to invent them where they are not.

There is a further complication in India, where many Gurung families have been settled for generations in Sikkim, Darjeeling and the north-east and where community records, school registers and local association lists are often better than any national table. Those records count members of particular organisations, not members of a people, and the two are not the same. Reading them well means asking who kept the list and why, which is the same discipline the rest of this book applies to every source.

The practical effect is that a Gurung reader looking for their own community's size will find three or four numbers and no way to reconcile them. The census figure is the most defensible for Nepal; the Indian figures are the least; the diaspora estimates are the most variable. A book that wants to be useful has to teach that distinction rather than pick the largest number and print it.

## [ne] नेपालबाहिर

नेपालबाहिर गन्दा अनुमानको भर पर्नुपर्छ। ठूला समुदाय भारतमा, विशेषगरी सिक्किम, दार्जिलिङ र पूर्वोत्तरमा, र बेलायत, हङकङ, अमेरिका तथा अन्य देशमा बस्छन्, तर ती सङ्ख्या फरक स्रोत र फरक वर्षबाट आउँछन् र फरक तरिकाले गनिएका हुन सक्छन्।

कसैले नागरिकता गन्छ, कसैले भाषा, कसैले संस्थाको सदस्यता। समुदायको अनुमानबाट बनेको अङ्कले कहिल्यै ल्होसारमा आएका सबैलाई गन्न सक्छ; राष्ट्रिय जनगणनाको अङ्कले फारममा नाम लेख्नेलाई मात्र। कुनै पनि गलत छैन; ती फरक प्रश्नका जवाफ हुन्, र गल्ती तिनलाई एउटै तालिकामा राखेर जोड्नु हो।

परिणाम यो हो: गुरुङको कुनै पनि विश्वव्यापी सङ्ख्या तथ्याङ्कको लुगा लगाएको अनुमान हो। जिम्मेवार वर्णनले "दसौँ हजार" भन्छ र कुन सर्वेक्षणबाट आयो भन्ने नाम लिन्छ। अजिम्मेवार वर्णनले "दस लाख" भन्छ र केही नाम लिँदैन, र त्यसपछि त्यो दोहोरिँदै तथ्यजस्तो देखिन्छ। यो अध्याय जहाँ नापिएको छ त्यहाँ सङ्ख्या प्रयोग गर्छ, र जहाँ नापिएको छैन त्यहाँ बनाउन इन्कार गर्छ।

भारतमा अझ एउटा जटिलता छ, जहाँ धेरै गुरुङ परिवार पुस्तौँदेखि सिक्किम, दार्जिलिङ र पूर्वोत्तरमा बसेका छन् र जहाँ सामुदायिक अभिलेख, विद्यालय-दर्ता र स्थानीय संस्थाका सूची कुनै राष्ट्रिय तालिकाभन्दा राम्रो हुन्छन्। ती अभिलेखले विशेष संस्थाका सदस्य गन्छन्, जनताका सदस्य होइन, र दुवै एउटै होइनन्। राम्रोसँग पढ्नु भनेको सूची कसले राख्यो र किन भन्ने सोध्नु हो, जो यो पुस्तकले हरेक स्रोतमा लगाउने अनुशासन हो।

व्यावहारिक असर यो हो: आफ्नै समुदायको आकार खोज्ने गुरुङ पाठकले तीन-चार सङ्ख्या भेट्छन् र मिलाउने बाटो भेट्दैनन्। नेपालका लागि जनगणनाको अङ्क सबैभन्दा रक्षायोग्य छ; भारतका अङ्क सबैभन्दा कम; प्रवासका अनुमान सबैभन्दा चञ्चल। उपयोगी हुन चाहने पुस्तकले सबैभन्दा ठूलो सङ्ख्या छानेर छाप्नुभन्दा त्यो भेद सिकाउनुपर्छ।

## [en] Where Gurungs live in Nepal

Read the census map and the community looks less like a territory than a set of nodes. Gurungs are concentrated in Gandaki Province and in the cities, and the distribution reflects the historic homeland, military recruitment, labour migration and the pull of towns.

Internal migration has been strong for decades. Villages lose young adults; towns gain them; a family may keep a house in the hills while living in Pokhara, returning for funerals and festivals, and may describe itself as living in both places. The result is a pattern that statistics record badly, because a person is counted where they slept on census night, not where their clan shrine is or where their grandparents are buried.

So the "Gurung area" is not a contiguous block of land any more, if it ever was. It is a network of villages, towns and diaspora destinations, joined by remittances, marriages and the calendar of Lhosar. A wedding in a village may bring guests from three countries; a funeral may be attended by a son who flew in from the Gulf and a daughter who joined by video call. The map of the community is a map of routes, and the census is only a photograph of where people happened to be standing.

Remittances are the mechanism that holds the network together. Money sent from a Gulf contract, a British care home or an Indian army pension pays school fees, builds a house that may stand empty for years, and funds the funeral that brings the family back. Towns grow because that money is spent in them, and villages persist because the same money maintains a house and a shrine. A household can therefore be spread across three countries and still function as one economic unit, which is a fact no census table records.

The towns in that network are not simply places people leave the village for. Pokhara and Kathmandu have their own Gurung institutions, their own Lhosar programmes and their own arrangements for funerals, and a family that has lived in town for two generations may still hold land, a house and a shrine obligation in the village. The relationship runs both ways: town money maintains the village, and the village gives the town family its claim to a clan and a name.

## [ne] नेपालमा गुरुङ कहाँ बस्छन्

जनगणनाको नक्सा पढ्दा समुदाय निरन्तर भूभागजस्तो होइन, जोडिएका केन्द्रजस्तो देखिन्छ। गुरुङ मुख्यतः गण्डकी प्रदेश र सहरमा केन्द्रित छन्, र यो वितरणले ऐतिहासिक मातृभूमि, सैनिक भर्ती, श्रम-बसाइँसराइ र सहरको तान देखाउँछ।

आन्तरिक बसाइँसराइ दशकौँदेखि बलियो छ। गाउँले युवा गुमाउँछन्; सहरले पाउँछ; परिवारले पहाडमा घर राख्दै पोखरामा बस्न सक्छ, अन्त्येष्टि र चाडमा फर्कंदै, र आफूलाई दुवै ठाउँमा बस्ने भन्न सक्छ। नतिजा यस्तो ढाँचा हो जो तथ्याङ्कले नराम्रोसँग अभिलेख गर्छ, किनकि मानिस जनगणनाको रात जहाँ सुत्यो त्यहाँ गनिन्छ, जहाँ उसको क्लेको देवस्थान छ वा हजुरबुबा गाडिएका छन् त्यहाँ होइन।

त्यसैले "गुरुङ क्षेत्र" अब एउटा निरन्तर भूभाग होइन, यदि कहिल्यै थियो भने। यो गाउँ, सहर र प्रवास-गन्तव्यको सञ्जाल हो, जो रेमिट्यान्स, विवाह र ल्होसारको पात्रोले जोडिएको छ। गाउँको विवाहमा तीन देशका पाहुना आउन सक्छन्; अन्त्येष्टिमा खाडीबाट उडेर आएको छोरा र भिडियो कलबाट जोडिएकी छोरी दुवै हुन सक्छन्। समुदायको नक्सा बाटोको नक्सा हो, र जनगणना त मानिस कहाँ उभिएका थिए भन्ने तस्बिर मात्र।

रेमिट्यान्स नै त्यो सञ्जाल जोडिराख्ने व्यवस्था हो। खाडीको करार, बेलायतको हेरचाह-घर वा भारतीय सैनिक पेन्सनबाट आएको पैसाले विद्यालय शुल्क तिर्छ, वर्षौँ खाली रहन सक्ने घर बनाउँछ, र परिवारलाई फर्काउने अन्त्येष्टिको खर्च जुटाउँछ। पैसा सहरमा खर्च हुँदा सहर बढ्छ, र त्यही पैसाले घर र देवस्थान धान्दा गाउँ टिक्छ। त्यसैले एउटा घर तीन देशमा छरिएर पनि एउटै आर्थिक इकाइका रूपमा चल्न सक्छ, जो कुनै जनगणना-तालिकामा अभिलेख हुँदैन।

त्यो सञ्जालका सहरहरू केवल गाउँ छोडेर जाने ठाउँ होइनन्। पोखरा र काठमाडौँका आफ्नै गुरुङ संस्था, आफ्नै ल्होसार कार्यक्रम र अन्त्येष्टिका आफ्नै व्यवस्था छन्, र दुई पुस्तादेखि सहरमा बसेको परिवारसँग अझै गाउँमा जग्गा, घर र देवस्थानको दायित्व हुन सक्छ। सम्बन्ध दुवैतिर चल्छ: सहरको पैसाले गाउँ धान्छ, र गाउँले सहरको परिवारलाई क्ले र नामको दाबी दिन्छ।

## [en] The diaspora numbers problem

Ask how many Gurung live outside Nepal and the honest answer is that nobody knows.

The Indian census does not enumerate Gurungs separately, though Sikkim, Darjeeling, Assam and the cantonment towns have large communities with long histories. Britain's Nepali population is estimated rather than counted, and the Gurung share of it is unknown. Hong Kong, the United States, Australia, Japan and the Gulf have smaller communities with no official statistics at all.

Community organisations produce estimates that range widely, inflated by enthusiasm or deflated by caution, and the numbers often serve an argument — about the size of a diaspora, the need for a community centre, the weight of a vote. Any global figure should therefore be treated as an order of magnitude, quoted with its source and year, and never used to win an argument.

What is not in doubt is what those people inherit. A language that fewer of them speak, a set of clans that still regulates who they may marry, and a ritual calendar that tells them when to come home. That inheritance is the subject of the chapters that follow, beginning with the question of where it came from.

The word "diaspora" itself needs care here. It is used for communities that have been abroad for five generations, such as those in Sikkim and the Indian cantonments, and for communities formed in the last thirty years, such as those in Britain and the Gulf. Their relationships to Nepal differ: one may hold citizenship and land, the other may never have seen the village its grandparents left. Counting them together produces a number that describes nothing in particular, which is one more reason this chapter declines to produce one.

Diaspora is a word that flatters some communities and misfiles others. A family in Sikkim whose ancestors arrived in the nineteenth century is not in the same situation as a family in Aldershot whose grandparents arrived in the 1980s, and neither is in the same situation as a labourer in the Gulf on a two-year contract who sends money home and intends to return. Any account that treats them as one population will get the numbers wrong and the experience wrong at the same time.

## [ne] प्रवासको सङ्ख्या-समस्या

नेपालबाहिर कति गुरुङ छन् भनेर सोध्नुहोस्, इमानदार जवाफ हो — कसैलाई थाहा छैन।

भारतीय जनगणनाले गुरुङलाई छुट्टै गन्दैन, यद्यपि सिक्किम, दार्जिलिङ, आसाम र छाउनी सहरमा लामो इतिहास भएका ठूला समुदाय छन्। बेलायतको नेपाली जनसङ्ख्या गनिएको होइन, अनुमान गरिएको हो, र त्यसमा गुरुङको अंश अज्ञात छ; हङकङ, अमेरिका, अस्ट्रेलिया, जापान र खाडीमा साना समुदाय छन्, जसको आधिकारिक तथ्याङ्क नै छैन।

सामुदायिक संस्थाका अनुमान फराकिलो दायरामा छन् — उत्साहले बढेको वा सतर्कताले घटेको — र ती सङ्ख्या प्रायः एउटा तर्कको सेवा गर्छन्: प्रवास कति ठूलो, सामुदायिक केन्द्र किन चाहिन्छ, कुन मतको कति तौल। त्यसैले विश्वव्यापी कुनै पनि सङ्ख्यालाई परिमाणको कोटि मान्नुपर्छ, स्रोत तथा वर्षसहित उद्धृत गर्नुपर्छ, र कहिल्यै तर्क जित्न प्रयोग गर्नु हुँदैन।

शङ्का नभएको कुरा ती मानिसले के पाइन्छ भन्ने हो। भाषा, जो उनीहरूमध्ये थोरैले बोल्छन्; क्ले-समूह, जसले अझै कससँग विवाह गर्ने तय गर्छ; र कर्मकाण्डी पात्रो, जसले कहिले घर फर्कने बताउँछ। त्यो उत्तराधिकार अबका अध्यायको विषय हो, र त्यो कहाँबाट आयो भन्ने प्रश्नबाट सुरु हुन्छ।

"प्रवास" शब्दलाई पनि यहाँ होसियारी चाहिन्छ। यो पाँच पुस्तादेखि विदेशमा भएका समुदायका लागि प्रयोग हुन्छ, जस्तै सिक्किम र भारतीय छाउनीका, र पछिल्लो तीस वर्षमा बनेका समुदायका लागि पनि, जस्तै बेलायत र खाडीका। नेपालसँग तिनको सम्बन्ध फरक छ: एउटासँग नागरिकता र जग्गा हुन सक्छ, अर्काले हजुरबुबाले छोडेको गाउँ कहिल्यै नदेखेको हुन सक्छ। तिनलाई सँगै गन्दा कुनै खास कुरा नबुझाउने सङ्ख्या बन्छ, जो यो अध्यायले सङ्ख्या नदिने अर्को कारण हो।

प्रवास शब्दले कुनै समुदायलाई चाप्लुसी गर्छ र कुनैलाई गलत फाइलमा राख्छ। सिक्किमको त्यो परिवार, जसका पुर्खा उन्नाइसौँ शताब्दीमा आए, Aldershot को त्यो परिवारसँग एउटै अवस्थामा छैन जसका हजुरबुबा १९८० को दशकमा आए, र दुवै खाडीमा दुई वर्षे करारमा गएर पैसा पठाउने र फर्कने मन गर्ने श्रमिकसँग एउटै अवस्थामा छैनन्। तिनलाई एउटै जनसङ्ख्या मान्ने कुनै पनि वर्णनले सङ्ख्या र अनुभव दुवै गलत गर्छ।
