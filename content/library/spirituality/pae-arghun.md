## [en] Why it matters

When someone dies, the question is not only where the body goes but where the soul goes. In Gurung belief a person's soul has to be guided on a journey to the realm of the ancestors after death, and a rite that is not performed properly is said to leave the soul wandering, which may bring trouble to the family.

That is the whole reason the rite exists, and it explains why families will spend money they do not have and wait months for the right person to conduct it. The rite is known as pae in Tamu Kyi and arghun or arghum in Nepali, and it is the largest ceremony a household will ever stage.

## [ne] किन महत्त्वपूर्ण छ

कसैको मृत्यु हुँदा प्रश्न शरीर कहाँ जान्छ भन्ने मात्र होइन, आत्मा कहाँ जान्छ भन्ने पनि हो। गुरुङ विश्वासमा मृत्युपछि मानिसको आत्मालाई पुर्खाको लोकसम्मको यात्रामा डोर्‍याउनुपर्छ, र राम्ररी नगरिएको विधिले आत्मा भौतारिने र परिवारलाई सताउन सक्ने भनिन्छ। यो विधि तामु क्यिमा प्ये र नेपालीमा अर्घुन वा अर्घुम भनिन्छ।

## [en] Who performs it

Ritual specialists such as the pachyu and the khlyepri (ghyabri) lead the ceremony with chants that describe the soul's path. The chant is a route: it names the places the soul must pass, the beings it will meet and the offerings that open each stage, and the specialist's authority rests on knowing it in the right order.

In some places Buddhist lamas take part or perform the rite instead, and Stan Mumford describes how, in Manang, these practitioners guide the soul and even speak with it through possession. The coexistence is not confusion. A household may hold a shamanic rite and a Buddhist one for the same death, because the two address different parts of the journey — and the family's obligation is to leave nothing undone.

## [ne] कसले गर्छ

पाच्यु र ख्ल्येप्री (घ्याब्री) जस्ता कर्मकाण्डी विशेषज्ञले आत्माको बाटो वर्णन गर्ने वाचनसहित विधि डोर्‍याउँछन्। कतै बौद्ध लामा सहभागी हुन्छन् वा विधि उनैले गर्छन्, र Stan Mumford ले मनाङमा यी विशेषज्ञले आत्मालाई डोर्‍याएर आवेशमा त्यससँग बोल्नेसम्म गरेको वर्णन गर्छन्।

## [en] A three-day gathering

Shanti Gurung describes the arghun as a three-day event that includes the syarga and klepri dances, performed in the name of the dead. Relatives travel from far away, bring offerings, and share food and labour, so the rite renews bonds between families as well as honouring the dead.

Three days is a minimum rather than a rule. The gathering has to feed everyone who comes, house them, and keep the ritual sequence moving through the night; the dances are performed by people who have a relationship to the dead, not by hired performers. A funeral is therefore also a public accounting of who belongs to whom, and a family with many obligations will have a large one.

## [ne] तीन दिनको भेला

Shanti Gurung ले अर्घुनलाई मृतकका नाममा गरिने स्यार्गा र क्लेप्री नाचसमेत भएको तीन दिने कार्यक्रम भन्छिन्। नातेदार टाढाबाट आउँछन्, चढावा ल्याउँछन्, र खाना तथा श्रम बाँड्छन्, त्यसैले विधिले मृतकको सम्मानजत्तिकै परिवारबीचको सम्बन्ध पनि नवीकरण गर्छ।

## [en] A note on respect

The chants and objects used in pae are sacred knowledge held by trained specialists. This page is a general introduction and does not reproduce ritual texts.

The restraint is deliberate. A chant that guides a soul is not a poem to be anthologised; it is property, authority and, in the specialists' understanding, a working instrument. Publishing a version would create a text that looks authoritative and has never been performed, and it would take from the people whose livelihood and standing depend on keeping it.

## [ne] सम्मानबारे एउटा टिप्पणी

प्येमा प्रयोग हुने वाचन र वस्तु तालिमप्राप्त विशेषज्ञले राख्ने पवित्र ज्ञान हुन्। यो पृष्ठ सामान्य परिचय मात्र हो र कर्मकाण्डी पाठ दोहोर्‍याउँदैन।

## [en] The funeral that lasts for days

A pae is not one ceremony but a sequence, and its length is a statement about the dead person and the household. The body is prepared and watched, kin are informed, and a specialist is called to establish what the dead require.

Depending on the family's means and the specialist's reading, rites may run for a night, for several days, or be deferred for months until the harvest, a son's return from abroad or the right lunar day. Each stage has its own offerings, recitations and people: the pachyu or klepri who conducts, the lama where a Buddhist ritual is included, the women who lament, the affines who arrive with grain and money. The final rites release the dead into the ancestral line and release the household from mourning, and only then can ordinary life — marriage negotiations, house repairs, the next festival — properly resume.

## [ne] दिनौँ चल्ने अन्त्येष्टि

प्ये एउटा कर्म होइन, क्रम हो, र त्यसको लम्बाइ मृतक र घरपरिवारबारे भनिएको कुरा हो। शरीर तयार गरेर पहरा दिइन्छ, नातेदारलाई खबर गरिन्छ, र मृतकलाई के चाहिन्छ स्थापित गर्न विशेषज्ञ बोलाइन्छ। परिवारको हैसियत र विशेषज्ञको पढाइअनुसार विधि एक रात, केही दिन, वा बाली भित्र्याउने, छोरा विदेशबाट फर्कने वा उचित चन्द्र-दिनसम्म महिनौँ सारिन सक्छ।

हरेक चरणका आफ्नै चढावा, वाचन र मानिस हुन्छन्: डोर्‍याउने पाच्यु वा क्लेप्री, बौद्ध विधि समावेश भए लामा, विलाप गर्ने महिला, अन्न र पैसा लिएर आउने सम्धी-पक्ष। अन्तिम विधिले मृतकलाई पुर्ख्यौली शृङ्खलामा छाड्छ र घरपरिवारलाई शोकबाट मुक्त गर्छ, र त्यसपछि मात्र विवाहको कुराकानी, घर मर्मत, अर्को चाडजस्ता साधारण जीवन ठीकसँग सुरु हुन सक्छ।

## [en] Who pays for a pae

A large funeral is expensive, and the cost is distributed in ways that record the family's place in a network. Sons and daughters contribute, affines bring grain, livestock and cash, village households send labour and food, and the specialist receives a fee that is often modest in cash but heavy in obligation.

Contributions are remembered, and a household that receives help at a death owes help at the next death in the giving family: a credit system without banks. Migration has complicated the arithmetic, because the person expected to pay may be in the Gulf or Britain and may return only for the final rites, sending money instead of presence. Funerals have therefore become both more expensive and more negotiated, and disagreements over who owes what are a familiar source of family tension.

## [ne] पाएको खर्च कसले बेहोर्छ

ठूलो अन्त्येष्टि महँगो हुन्छ, र खर्च बाँड्ने तरिकाले परिवारको सञ्जालमा स्थान अभिलेख गर्छ। छोराछोरीले योगदान दिन्छन्, सम्धी-पक्षले अन्न, पशु र नगद ल्याउँछन्, गाउँका घरले श्रम र खाना पठाउँछन्, र विशेषज्ञले नगदमा थोरै तर दायित्वमा भारी पारिश्रमिक पाउँछन्। योगदान सम्झिन्छ, र मृत्युमा सहयोग पाएको घरले दिने परिवारको अर्को मृत्युमा सहयोग गर्नुपर्छ: बैंकबिनाको ऋण-प्रणाली।

बसाइँसराइले हिसाब जटिल बनायो, किनकि खर्च बेहोर्नुपर्ने मानिस खाडी वा बेलायतमा हुन सक्छ र अन्तिम विधिमा मात्र फर्केर उपस्थितिको सट्टा पैसा पठाउन सक्छ। त्यसैले अन्त्येष्टि अझ महँगो र अझ मोलमोलाइको विषय बनेका छन्, र कसले के तिर्नुपर्छ भन्ने असहमति पारिवारिक तनावको परिचित स्रोत हो।
