## [en] Kohla, the Three Villages

The Cambridge record of Evans's research calls Kohla Sombre — Kohla, the Three Villages — an ancestral settlement significant to the Tamu community, one that both hosted and instigated the project.

That last detail is easy to pass over and should not be. The village was not merely a site chosen by a university; it was a place whose own people wanted the work done, and the questions the project asked were shaped by that. When a community invites an excavation, it is usually asking for something specific: evidence that it was here, a date it can point to, a rebuttal of the story that it arrived late or from elsewhere.

The study brought settlement and fort surveys into conversation with ethnography and oral accounts, which is a rarer combination than it sounds. An archaeologist's wall, an elder's genealogy and a British library's file can describe the same ridge, and the value of the project lay in putting them side by side rather than in any one of them.

The work did not run to completion, and that matters for everything read here.

A community-invited project is a different kind of research from a project that arrives. The questions are negotiated, the finds are interpreted with the village present, and the results belong to people who will still be there when the archaeologists leave. That does not make the work less rigorous; it makes it accountable in a way that a trench in an empty valley is not.

The fort surveys are the part of the work that speaks most directly to the confederation. A fort on a ridge is not a palace; it is a piece of infrastructure that only makes sense if there is an organisation capable of manning it, provisioning it and agreeing who commands it. Finding one is finding a political fact, even when it cannot be dated.

## [ne] कोह्ला, तीन गाउँ

Evans को अनुसन्धानको क्याम्ब्रिज अभिलेखले कोह्ला सोम्ब्रे — कोह्ला, तीन गाउँ — लाई तामु समुदायका लागि महत्त्वपूर्ण पुर्ख्यौली बस्ती भन्छ, जसले परियोजनालाई आयोजना पनि गर्‍यो र आतिथ्य पनि दियो।

त्यो अन्तिम कुरा सजिलै छुट्न सक्छ र छुट्नु हुँदैन। गाउँ विश्वविद्यालयले छानेको स्थल मात्र थिएन; त्यो त्यस्तो ठाउँ थियो जसका आफ्नै मानिस काम गराउन चाहन्थे, र परियोजनाका प्रश्न त्यसैले आकार पाएका थिए। समुदायले उत्खनन बोलाउँदा प्रायः केही खास कुरा मागिरहेको हुन्छ: यो यहाँ थियो भन्ने प्रमाण, देखाउन मिल्ने मिति, ढिलो वा अन्त कतैबाट आयो भन्ने कथाको खण्डन।

अध्ययनले बस्ती र किल्लाका सर्वेक्षणलाई नृवंश-विवरण र मौखिक विवरणसँग संवादमा ल्यायो, जो सुन्दा जति सामान्य छ त्यति छैन। पुरातत्त्वविद्को पर्खाल, वृद्धको वंशावली र बेलायती पुस्तकालयको फाइलले एउटै डाँडा वर्णन गर्न सक्छन्, र परियोजनाको मूल्य तिनलाई सँगै राख्नुमा थियो, कुनै एउटामा होइन।

काम अन्तसम्म चल्न सकेन, र यहाँ पढिने सबै कुराका लागि त्यो महत्त्वपूर्ण छ।

समुदायले बोलाएको परियोजना आफैँ पुगेको परियोजनाभन्दा फरक अनुसन्धान हो। प्रश्न मोलमोलाइ हुन्छन्, फेला गाउँको उपस्थितिमा व्याख्या गरिन्छ, र नतिजा पुरातत्त्वविद् फर्केपछि पनि त्यहीँ रहने मानिसको हुन्छ। यसले कामलाई कम कठोर बनाउँदैन; खाली उपत्यकाको खाडलभन्दा फरक तरिकाले जवाफदेह बनाउँछ।

किल्ला-सर्वेक्षण संघसँग सबैभन्दा सिधा बोल्ने भाग हो। डाँडाको किल्ला दरबार होइन; त्यो पूर्वाधार हो जसको अर्थ तब मात्र हुन्छ जब त्यसलाई चलाउन, खुवाउन र कसले कमाण्ड गर्ने तय गर्न सक्ने संगठन होस्। एउटा भेट्टाउनु राजनीतिक तथ्य भेट्टाउनु हो, मिति तोक्न नसकिए पनि।

## [en] An interrupted investigation

The project completed one excavation season before political insurgency interrupted it.

One season is enough to establish a sequence of occupation and to recover pottery, bone and building traces; it is not enough to date a founding, still less to reconstruct the history of a settlement. A single season also produces a sample, and a sample from one trench in one village is a small window on a large landscape.

Physical remains and community interpretations therefore cannot be condensed into a definitive founding date or a fully reconstructed history of the settlement. What can be said is bounded by one season's evidence, and the account says so rather than filling the gap with inference. That restraint is not a failure of the research; it is what the research actually produced, and a reader is better served by it than by a confident story built on a season's pottery.

A reader who wants a date will not find one here. That absence is itself part of the record, and it is the reason the next chapter's confederation is described in the terms it is.

A second season would have changed what could be said. It would have allowed a wider trench, a larger sample and a comparison between the settlement and the fort; it might have produced the radiocarbon dates that a founding claim needs. What exists instead is a fragment, published honestly as a fragment, and that is the material any later account has to work from.

The interruption was not a delay in the ordinary sense. When a project stops, the records are archived, the permits lapse and the relationships with the village have to be rebuilt by whoever comes next. What survives is a season's notebook and a set of finds, which is more than nothing and less than the study the community had asked for.

## [ne] अवरुद्ध अन्वेषण

परियोजनाले राजनीतिक विद्रोहले रोकिनुअघि एउटा उत्खनन-सिजन पूरा गर्‍यो।

एउटा सिजन बसोबासको क्रम स्थापित गर्न र माटाका भाँडा, हड्डी तथा भवनका चिन्ह निकाल्न पर्याप्त छ; स्थापनाको मिति तोक्न पर्याप्त छैन, बस्तीको इतिहास पुनर्निर्माण गर्न झन् होइन। एउटा सिजनले नमुना पनि दिन्छ, र एउटा गाउँको एउटा खाडलको नमुना ठूलो भूदृश्यमा सानो झ्याल हो।

त्यसैले भौतिक अवशेष र सामुदायिक व्याख्यालाई निश्चित स्थापना-मिति वा बस्तीको पूरा पुनर्निर्मित इतिहासमा खुम्च्याउन सकिँदैन। भन्न सकिने कुरा एउटा सिजनको प्रमाणले सीमित छ, र यो विवरणले त्यही भन्छ, खाली ठाउँ अनुमानले भर्दैन। त्यो संयम अनुसन्धानको असफलता होइन; अनुसन्धानले वास्तवमा निकालेको कुरा त्यही हो, र एउटा सिजनको भाँडामा बनेको आत्मविश्वासी कथाभन्दा पाठकलाई त्यसैले बढी फाइदा हुन्छ।

मिति खोज्ने पाठकले यहाँ भेट्नुहुनेछैन। त्यो अभाव आफैँ अभिलेखको भाग हो, र त्यही कारण अर्को अध्यायको संघलाई त्यस्तै शब्दमा वर्णन गरिएको छ।

दोस्रो सिजनले भन्न सकिने कुरा बदल्थ्यो। यसले फराकिलो खाडल, ठूलो नमुना र बस्ती तथा किल्लाबीचको तुलना दिन्थ्यो; स्थापना-दाबीलाई चाहिने रेडियोकार्बन मिति पनि निकाल्न सक्थ्यो। त्यसको सट्टा खण्ड छ, इमानदारीसाथ खण्डका रूपमा प्रकाशित, र पछिको कुनै पनि विवरणले त्यही सामग्रीबाट काम गर्नुपर्छ।

अवरोध साधारण ढिलाइ थिएन। परियोजना रोकिँदा अभिलेख अभिलेखालयमा जान्छ, अनुमति सकिन्छ, र गाउँसँगको सम्बन्ध अर्को आउनेले फेरि बनाउनुपर्छ। बाँच्ने कुरा एउटा सिजनको नोटबुक र फेला परेका वस्तु हुन्, जो शून्यभन्दा बढी र समुदायले मागेको अध्ययनभन्दा कम हो।

## [en] Names and evidence

Kohla and Khola Sothar are names encountered in wider discussion of ancestral places, and a complete account of their local spellings, pronunciations and geographical equivalences needs community verification.

Names that travel through three languages and two centuries of writing arrive in English with their vowels rearranged, and the version in a book is rarely the version on a signboard. "Kohla" and "Khola" may be the same place under two transcriptions, or two places whose names were confused by a clerk; the difference matters to the people who live there and is not resolvable from a library.

One caution belongs here too. No unrelated ruins or mountain photographs are presented here as photographs of Kohla. A hill village is easy to illustrate and hard to illustrate truthfully, and a generic picture labelled with a real name is a small lie that spreads: within a year it is on three websites, and within five it is evidence. The second name, though, leads somewhere larger than a village.

Transcription is where local knowledge and scholarly convention meet, and the meeting is usually uneven. A researcher writes what they hear through the phonology of their own language; a clerk writes what fits the registry; a signboard follows neither. Recovering the local form requires asking people who live there, which is why this chapter marks the names as needing verification rather than settling them.

None of this makes the names unusable. It makes them provisional, in the way that all place names are provisional when they have crossed a language boundary: usable for an argument, unreliable as an identifier, and best accompanied by the local form when it can be obtained.

## [ne] नाम र प्रमाण

कोह्ला र खोला सोथार पुर्ख्यौली ठाउँबारेको फराकिलो बहसमा भेटिने नाम हुन्, र तिनका स्थानीय हिज्जे, उच्चारण र भौगोलिक समताका लागि सामुदायिक पुष्टि चाहिन्छ।

तीन भाषा र दुई शताब्दीको लेखाइ पार गरेका नाम अङ्ग्रेजीमा स्वर मिलाएर आउँछन्, र किताबको संस्करण साइनबोर्डको संस्करण विरलै हुन्छ। "कोह्ला" र "खोला" दुई ट्रान्सक्रिप्शनमा एउटै ठाउँ हुन सक्छन्, वा कर्मचारीले नाम मिलाइदिएका दुई ठाउँ; भेद त्यहाँ बस्नेका लागि महत्त्वपूर्ण छ र पुस्तकालयबाट टुङ्ग्याउन सकिँदैन।

यहाँ एउटा होसियारी पनि पर्छ। यहाँ कुनै असम्बन्धित भग्नावशेष वा पहाडको तस्बिर कोह्लाको तस्बिर भनेर राखिएको छैन। पहाडी गाउँको चित्र देखाउन सजिलो र सत्यतापूर्वक देखाउन गाह्रो हुन्छ, र वास्तविक नाम लेखिएको सामान्य तस्बिर एउटा सानो झुट हो जो फैलिन्छ: वर्षभित्र तीन वेबसाइटमा पुग्छ, पाँच वर्षभित्र प्रमाण बन्छ। दोस्रो नाम भने गाउँभन्दा ठूलो कतैतिर लैजान्छ।

ट्रान्सक्रिप्शन स्थानीय ज्ञान र विद्वत्तापूर्ण परम्परा भेट्ने ठाउँ हो, र भेट प्रायः असमान हुन्छ। अनुसन्धानकर्ताले आफ्नै भाषाको ध्वनि-प्रणालीबाट सुनेको कुरा लेख्छ; कर्मचारीले दर्तामा मिल्ने कुरा; साइनबोर्डले कुनैलाई पछ्याउँदैन। स्थानीय रूप निकाल्न त्यहाँ बस्नेलाई सोध्नुपर्छ, त्यसैले यो अध्यायले नामलाई टुङ्ग्याउनुभन्दा पुष्टि चाहिने भनेर चिन्ह लगाएको छ।

यसले नामलाई अप्रयोगी बनाउँदैन। अस्थायी बनाउँछ, जसरी भाषा-सीमा पार गरेका सबै ठाउँका नाम अस्थायी हुन्छन्: तर्कका लागि प्रयोगयोग्य, पहिचानका लागि अविश्वसनीय, र स्थानीय रूप भेटिए सँगै राख्दा राम्रो।

## [en] Seventeen ridges, one history

Khola Sothar — "seventeen rivers" or "seventeen ridges", depending on how the term is read — names a confederation of Gurung areas rather than a single territory.

Its member groups were distributed across the hills of central Nepal, and its business was collective: settling disputes, organising defence, regulating relations between clans and presenting a common front to neighbouring powers. Those are the four things a confederation of villages actually needs to do when there is no king to do them.

It was not a state with a capital and a bureaucracy, and it did not survive as one. There was no standing army, no treasury, no written constitution; there were councils, elders and agreements that were renewed when they were needed. What it left behind is a political vocabulary — that Gurung areas share interests, that decisions can be made collectively, that a council of elders can speak for more than one village — and modern organisations draw on that vocabulary even when their structure is entirely new.

A confederation, unlike a kingdom, is remembered as an idea rather than a dynasty. That is why it can be invoked two centuries later without anyone having to claim descent from a ruler.

The four functions of the confederation are worth spelling out because each leaves a different trace. Disputes leave genealogies and memories of settlements; defence leaves forts and stories of raids; the regulation of clan relations leaves marriage rules that can still be observed; and a common front leaves the names of neighbouring powers in the record. None of these is a constitution, and together they describe a working polity.

Defence is the function that leaves the most visible trace, because a confederation that had to defend itself built something. Forts on ridges, watch posts above valleys, walls across a spur: the survey recorded structures of this kind, and their position tells you what the confederation thought it needed to watch. None of them is a capital, and all of them are evidence of organisation.

## [ne] सत्र क्षेत्र, एउटै इतिहास

खोला सोथार — "सत्र नदी" वा "सत्र डाँडा", शब्द कसरी पढिन्छ भन्नेमा — एउटै भूभाग होइन, गुरुङ क्षेत्रहरूको संघको नाम हो।

यसका सदस्य समूह मध्य नेपालका पहाडमा छरिएका थिए, र यसको काम सामूहिक थियो: विवाद टुङ्ग्याउने, रक्षा संगठित गर्ने, क्लेबीचको सम्बन्ध नियमित गर्ने र छिमेकी शक्तिसामु एउटै मोर्चा देखाउने। राजा नहुँदा गाउँको संघले वास्तवमा गर्नुपर्ने चार काम तिनै हुन्।

यो राजधानी र कर्मचारीतन्त्र भएको राज्य थिएन, र त्यसरी बाँचेन। स्थायी सेना थिएन, कोष थिएन, लिखित संविधान थिएन; परिषद्, वृद्ध र आवश्यक पर्दा नवीकरण हुने सहमति थिए। यसले छाडेको कुरा राजनीतिक शब्दावली हो — गुरुङ क्षेत्रका साझा स्वार्थ छन्, निर्णय सामूहिक हुन सक्छ, वृद्धको परिषद्ले एकभन्दा बढी गाउँका लागि बोल्न सक्छ — र आधुनिक संस्थाले आफ्नो ढाँचा पूरै नयाँ हुँदाहुँदै पनि त्यही शब्दावली प्रयोग गर्छन्।

राज्यभन्दा फरक, संघ वंशजका रूपमा होइन, विचारका रूपमा सम्झिन्छ। त्यसैले दुई शताब्दीपछि पनि कसैले शासकबाट वंश दाबी नगरी यसलाई उद्धृत गर्न सक्छ।

संघका चार काम खोल्नु जरुरी छ किनकि हरेकले फरक चिन्ह छाड्छ। विवादले वंशावली र टुङ्गोको सम्झना छाड्छ; रक्षाले किल्ला र आक्रमणका कथा; क्ले-सम्बन्धको नियमनले अझै देखिने विवाह-नियम; र साझा मोर्चाले अभिलेखमा छिमेकी शक्तिका नाम। यीमध्ये कुनै संविधान होइन, र सँगै तिनले चल्ने राजनीतिक इकाइ वर्णन गर्छन्।

रक्षा सबैभन्दा देखिने चिन्ह छाड्ने काम हो, किनकि आफ्नो रक्षा गर्नुपर्ने संघले केही बनाएको हुन्छ। डाँडामा किल्ला, उपत्यकामाथि पहरा-चौकी, डाँडो पार गर्ने पर्खाल: सर्वेक्षणले यस्ता संरचना अभिलेख गर्‍यो, र तिनको स्थानले संघले के हेर्नुपर्ने ठान्यो भन्ने बताउँछ। तीमध्ये कुनै राजधानी होइन, र सबै संगठनको प्रमाण हुन्।

## [en] Names on a map and names in a story

The seventeen names are remembered in different orders and different spellings, and local accounts disagree about which places belong.

That is normal for a confederation of villages and clan territories rather than surveyed districts. The list was a political instrument and changed as the balance of power changed: a village that grew might be added, a village that declined might be forgotten, and a family that married into a stronger neighbour might remember its membership differently.

Historians have matched the names to modern locations with partial success, and the exercise matters because land, grazing and marriage rights were tied to membership. A place that can claim membership can claim a share of the confederation's history, so the disagreement is not pedantry; it is a dispute about who belongs to the political community Khola Sothar represents.

Names on a map and names in a story are two different kinds of claim, and this place holds both at once. The consequence is that any list printed here has to be presented as a record of claims rather than as a register of members, which is how the older accounts themselves treat it.

Membership had material consequences. Grazing rights, access to a forest, the right to settle a household on a slope: all of these followed from belonging to a place that belonged to the confederation. That is why the list of seventeen is argued over with such precision, and why a village's claim to be on it is a claim about resources and not only about prestige.

A list that changes is still a list with rules. New members could be admitted, old ones could lapse, and a village could belong through a clan rather than through its own headman. That flexibility is what allowed the confederation to last as long as it did, and it is also what makes a definitive reconstruction impossible from the surviving names alone.

## [ne] नक्साका नाम र कथाका नाम

सत्र नाम फरक क्रम र फरक हिज्जेमा सम्झिन्छन्, र कुन ठाउँ पर्छ भन्नेमा स्थानीय विवरण असहमत छन्।

सर्वेक्षण गरिएका जिल्लाभन्दा गाउँ र क्ले-क्षेत्रको संघका लागि त्यो सामान्य हो। सूची राजनीतिक हतियार थियो र शक्ति-सन्तुलनसँगै बदलिन्थ्यो: ठूलो भएको गाउँ थपिन सक्थ्यो, घटेको बिर्सिन सक्थ्यो, र बलियो छिमेकीमा विवाह गरेको परिवारले आफ्नो सदस्यता फरक तरिकाले सम्झिन सक्थ्यो।

इतिहासकारले नामलाई आधुनिक स्थानसँग आंशिक सफलतासहित मिलाएका छन्, र त्यो अभ्यास महत्त्वपूर्ण छ किनकि जग्गा, चरन र विवाह-अधिकार सदस्यतासँग जोडिएका थिए। सदस्यता दाबी गर्ने ठाउँले संघको इतिहासको हिस्सा दाबी गर्न सक्छ, त्यसैले असहमति शास्त्रीयता होइन; खोला सोथारले प्रतिनिधित्व गर्ने राजनीतिक समुदायमा को पर्छ भन्ने विवाद हो।

नक्साका नाम र कथाका नाम दुई फरक किसिमका दाबी हुन्, र यो ठाउँ दुवै एकैचोटि बोक्छ। परिणाम यो हो: यहाँ छापिने कुनै पनि सूची सदस्यको दर्ता होइन, दाबीको अभिलेखका रूपमा प्रस्तुत गर्नुपर्छ, र पुराना विवरणले पनि त्यसै गरेका छन्।

सदस्यताका भौतिक परिणाम थिए। चरनको अधिकार, वनमा पहुँच, भिरालोमा घर बसाउने हक: सबै संघको भएको ठाउँको सदस्यताबाट आउँथे। त्यसैले सत्रको सूचीमा यति सूक्ष्मतासाथ बहस हुन्छ, र गाउँको सूचीमा पर्ने दाबी प्रतिष्ठाको मात्र होइन, स्रोतको दाबी हो।

बदलिने सूची पनि नियम भएको सूची हो। नयाँ सदस्य थपिन सक्थे, पुराना हट्न सक्थे, र गाउँ आफ्नै मुखियाबाट होइन, क्लेबाट सदस्य हुन सक्थ्यो। त्यो लचिलोपनले नै संघलाई यति लामो बनायो, र त्यही कारण बाँकी नामबाट निश्चित पुनर्निर्माण असम्भव छ।

## [en] The confederation in modern politics

Twentieth-century Gurung organisations, and the identity movement after 1990, repeatedly invoked Khola Sothar as evidence that the community had its own political tradition and did not need to be taught how to govern itself.

The reference appears in speeches, organisational names and the historical sections of community publications, and in arguments about federalism, where the old confederation is cited as a precedent for autonomy. A community that can point to a council of elders deciding matters across seventeen ridges has an answer to anyone who says it never governed anything.

Whether it functioned as those arguments imply is a separate question on which historians are cautious. A confederation of ridges that met to settle disputes is not a province with a budget, and the gap between the two is exactly where political memory does its work. But the political use of the past is itself a fact, and it shows what the community wants its history to authorise.

From a confederation of ridges the book moves to one of its villages, and to the question of what happens to a place when the world arrives to look at it.

Federalism made the confederation newly relevant, because a country reorganising its provinces needs precedents for how hill areas might govern themselves. Whether Khola Sothar offers such a precedent is arguable; that it is cited as one is not. The citation tells you what the community wants its past to authorise, which is a fact about the present that a history of the eighteenth century cannot supply.

## [ne] आधुनिक राजनीतिमा संघ

बीसौँ शताब्दीका गुरुङ संस्था र सन् १९९० पछिको पहिचान आन्दोलनले खोला सोथारलाई समुदायको आफ्नै राजनीतिक परम्परा छ र आफूलाई शासन गर्न सिकाउनु पर्दैन भन्ने प्रमाणका रूपमा बारम्बार उद्धृत गरे।

त्यो सन्दर्भ भाषण, संस्थाका नाम र सामुदायिक प्रकाशनका ऐतिहासिक खण्डमा, र संघीयताबारेको बहसमा आउँछ, जहाँ पुरानो संघलाई स्वायत्तताको नजिर भनेर उद्धृत गरिन्छ। सत्र डाँडापारिका विषय वृद्धको परिषद्ले टुङ्ग्याएको देखाउन सक्ने समुदायसँग कहिल्यै केही शासन गरेन भन्ने जोकोहीलाई जवाफ हुन्छ।

ती तर्कले देखाएझैँ यसले काम गर्‍यो कि भन्ने अलग प्रश्न हो, जसमा इतिहासकार सतर्क छन्। विवाद टुङ्ग्याउन भेला हुने डाँडाको संघ बजेट भएको प्रदेश होइन, र दुईबीचको दूरीमा नै राजनीतिक सम्झनाले काम गर्छ। तर विगतको राजनीतिक प्रयोग आफैँ तथ्य हो, र यसले समुदायले आफ्नो इतिहासबाट के अधिकार दिलाउन चाहन्छ भन्ने देखाउँछ।

संघीयताले संघलाई फेरि सान्दर्भिक बनायो, किनकि प्रदेश पुनर्गठन गर्ने देशलाई पहाडी क्षेत्रले कसरी शासन गर्न सक्छ भन्ने नजिर चाहिन्छ। खोला सोथारले त्यस्तो नजिर दिन्छ कि भन्ने बहसयोग्य छ; नजिरका रूपमा उद्धृत हुन्छ भन्ने होइन। त्यो उद्धरणले समुदायले आफ्नो विगतबाट के अधिकार दिलाउन चाहन्छ देखाउँछ, जो वर्तमानको तथ्य हो र अठारौँ शताब्दीको इतिहासले दिन सक्दैन।

डाँडाको संघबाट पुस्तक त्यसका एउटा गाउँतिर जान्छ, र संसार हेर्न आउँदा ठाउँलाई के हुन्छ भन्ने प्रश्नतिर।
