The Cambridge record of Evans’s research describes Kohla Sombre—Kohla, the Three Villages—as an ancestral settlement significant to the Tamu community that hosted and instigated the project. The study brought settlement and fort surveys into conversation with ethnography and oral accounts.

## [en] Seventeen ridges, one history

Khola Sothar — ‘seventeen rivers’ or ‘seventeen ridges’ depending on how the term is read — names a confederation of Gurung areas rather than a single territory. Its member groups were distributed across the hills of central Nepal, and the confederation’s business was collective: settling disputes, organising defence, regulating relations between clans and presenting a common front to neighbouring powers. It was not a state with a capital and a bureaucracy, and it did not survive as one. What it left behind is a political vocabulary: the idea that Gurung areas share interests, that decisions can be made collectively, and that a council of elders can speak for more than one village. Modern organisations draw on that vocabulary even when their structure is entirely new.

## [en] Names on a map and names in a story

The seventeen names are remembered in different orders and with different spellings, and local accounts disagree about which places belong. This is normal for a confederation whose members were villages and clan territories rather than surveyed districts; the list was a political instrument, and it changed as the balance of power changed. Historians have tried to match the names to modern locations, with partial success. The exercise matters because land, grazing and marriage rights were tied to membership, and because a place that can claim membership can claim a share of the confederation’s history. The disagreement is therefore not pedantry: it is a dispute about who belongs to the political community that Khola Sothar represents.

सत्र नाम फरक क्रम र फरक हिज्जेमा सम्झिन्छन्, र स्थानीय विवरण कुन ठाउँ सदस्य हुन् भन्नेमा असहमत छन्। सदस्य गाउँ र क्ले-क्षेत्र भएको संघका लागि यो सामान्य हो; सूची राजनीतिक औजार थियो, र शक्तिको सन्तुलन बदलिँदै जाँदा बदलियो। इतिहासकारले नामलाई आधुनिक स्थानसँग मिलाउन खोजेका छन्, आंशिक सफलतासहित। यो अभ्यास महत्त्वपूर्ण छ किनकि जग्गा, चरन र विवाहको अधिकार सदस्यतासँग जोडिएको थियो, र सदस्यता दाबी गर्ने ठाउँले संघको इतिहासमा हिस्सा दाबी गर्न सक्छ। त्यसैले असहमति खाली कुरा होइन: यो खोला सोर्थरले प्रतिनिधित्व गर्ने राजनीतिक समुदायमा को पर्छ भन्ने विवाद हो।

## [en] The confederation in modern politics

Twentieth-century Gurung organisations, and the identity movement after 1990, repeatedly invoked Khola Sothar as evidence that the community had its own political tradition and did not need to be taught how to govern itself. The reference appears in speeches, in organisational names and in the historical sections of community publications. It also appears in arguments about federalism, where the old confederation is cited as a precedent for autonomous arrangements. Whether the historical confederation actually functioned as those arguments imply is a separate question, and historians are cautious; but the political use of the past is itself a fact, and it tells us what the community wants its history to authorise.
