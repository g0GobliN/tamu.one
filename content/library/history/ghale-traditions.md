## [en] Remembering local authority

Mani Raj Gurung discusses Ghale rulers within a wider interpretation of power, knowledge and changes in Gurung identity. This is an author’s analytical framework, not proof of a single uniformly documented kingdom across every Gurung region.

## [en] Ruins are not a royal chronology

Evans’s Kohla research offers an archaeological and ethnohistorical setting for examining ancestral leadership traditions. Identifying a structure in local memory as a king’s house does not by itself establish the identity, reign dates or territorial extent of a ruler.

## [en] Ghale and Gurung: a disputed boundary

The relationship between Ghale and Gurung is one of the most argued questions in the ethnography of the region. Some accounts treat Ghale as a rank or a ruling lineage within Gurung society; others treat Ghale as a separate group with its own language, clans and history; still others describe a process by which a ruling house was gradually absorbed into the wider Gurung category. Nineteenth-century observers were inconsistent, and modern census categories do not settle the matter because people choose how to record themselves. The disagreement matters because it concerns political history — who ruled whom — as much as ethnic classification, and because land, marriage and ritual rights can depend on which account a family accepts.

## [ne] घले र गुरुङ: विवादित सीमा

घले र गुरुङको सम्बन्ध यस क्षेत्रको नृवंश-विवरणमा सबैभन्दा बहस भएका प्रश्नमध्ये एक हो। कुनै विवरणले घलेलाई गुरुङ समाजभित्रको दर्जा वा शासक वंश मान्छ; कुनैले घलेलाई आफ्नै भाषा, क्ले र इतिहास भएको छुट्टै समूह मान्छ; अरूले शासक घराना क्रमशः फराकिलो गुरुङ कोटिमा समाहित हुने प्रक्रिया वर्णन गर्छन्। उन्नाइसौँ शताब्दीका पर्यवेक्षक असंगत थिए, र आधुनिक जनगणना-कोटिले पनि विषय टुङ्ग्याउँदैन किनकि मानिस आफैँ आफूलाई कसरी रेकर्ड गर्ने छान्छन्। असहमति महत्त्वपूर्ण छ किनकि यो जातीय वर्गीकरणजत्तिकै राजनीतिक इतिहास — कसले कसलाई शासन गर्‍यो — सँग पनि सम्बन्धित छ, र जग्गा, विवाह तथा कर्मकाण्डी अधिकार परिवारले कुन विवरण स्वीकार्छ भन्नेमा निर्भर हुन सक्छ।

## [en] The king who is remembered

Ghale traditions remember a king or a ruling house, and the memory is preserved in stories about a palace, a shrine, a grant of land or a founding ancestor. The details vary by locality, and the same figure may appear under different names in different valleys. Historians have tried to connect these traditions to the documented hill states and to the genealogies kept by families, with limited success; the evidence supports the existence of chiefly houses and their incorporation into larger polities, but not a single royal chronology. The memory is nevertheless socially real: families who claim descent from the house carry prestige, ritual duties and marriage expectations that are acknowledged by their neighbours.

## [ne] सम्झिएका राजा

घले परम्पराले राजा वा शासक घरानालाई सम्झन्छ, र त्यो सम्झना दरबार, देवालय, जग्गा-दान वा संस्थापक पुर्खाबारेका कथामा जोगिएको छ। विवरण ठाउँअनुसार फरक पर्छ, र एउटै व्यक्ति फरक उपत्यकामा फरक नामले देखिन सक्छन्। इतिहासकारले यी परम्परालाई अभिलेख भएका पहाडी राज्य र परिवारका वंशावलीसँग जोड्न खोजेका छन्, सीमित सफलतासहित; प्रमाणले मुखिया-घरानाको अस्तित्व र ठूला राज्यमा तिनको समावेशलाई समर्थन गर्छ, तर एउटै राजकीय कालक्रमलाई होइन। सम्झना तैपनि सामाजिक रूपमा वास्तविक छ: घरानाबाट आएको दाबी गर्ने परिवारले प्रतिष्ठा, कर्मकाण्डी जिम्मेवारी र विवाहको अपेक्षा बोक्छन्, जो छिमेकीले स्वीकार गर्छन्।

## [en] Ritual traces of a ruling house

Some ritual practices are explained by reference to the old ruling house: a particular offering made at a particular shrine, a dance performed only in certain villages, a prohibition observed by certain clans. These traces are difficult to date and easy to over-interpret, but they are the kind of evidence from which the political history of a region can be reconstructed when documents are missing. They also show how ritual can preserve a claim to status long after the political structure that supported it has gone. For the community, the question is not only what happened but what the memory of it obliges: who keeps the shrine, who leads the dance, and who is entitled to be described as its heir.

## [ne] शासक घरानाका कर्मकाण्डी चिन्ह

केही कर्मकाण्डी अभ्यास पुरानो शासक घरानाको सन्दर्भले व्याख्या गरिन्छ: कुनै देवालयमा गरिने विशेष अर्पण, केवल केही गाउँमा प्रस्तुत गरिने नाच, केही क्लेले पालना गर्ने निषेध। यी चिन्ह मिति गर्न गाह्रो र बढी व्याख्या गर्न सजिलो छ, तर दस्तावेज नभएका बेला क्षेत्रको राजनीतिक इतिहास पुनर्निर्माण गर्ने यही किसिमका प्रमाण हुन्। तिनले यो पनि देखाउँछन् — कर्मकाण्डले समर्थन गर्ने राजनीतिक संरचना गएपछि पनि दर्जाको दाबी कसरी जोगाउन सक्छ। समुदायका लागि प्रश्न के भयो मात्र होइन, त्यसको सम्झनाले के दायित्व दिन्छ पनि हो: देवालय कसले राख्छ, नाच कसले नेतृत्व गर्छ, र त्यसको उत्तराधिकारी भनिने अधिकार कसलाई छ।
