## [en] Languages are not populations

Open a language map of Nepal and Gurung sits inside the Sino-Tibetan family — in Glottolog's classification, beside Tamang and Thakali. The map is a diagram of relatedness among languages, and it is easy to mistake it for a diagram of relatedness among peoples.

It is not. A language family says that these languages descend from a common ancestor at some depth of time, and that the speakers of the ancestor languages were in contact, or were the same people, at some point long before anyone wrote anything down. It does not say where those speakers lived, when they separated, or that anyone today is the direct descendant of the people who spoke the ancestor. Languages can spread across a population without replacing it, and populations can change language without moving at all.

The map invites exactly that mistake, which is why the first thing this chapter has to do is refuse it. A Gurung reader looking for origins will find a family tree, and the tree is real; what is not real is the straight line from a branch on the tree to a village in Lamjung. The next question is therefore not "where on the tree" but "what kind of evidence could say more".

The misreading is common enough to have a shape. A reader sees Gurung placed beside Tamang and Thakali, concludes that the three were once one people, and then looks for the valley where that people lived. Nepal's linguistic surveys encourage the mistake by presenting families as trees with branches, which is accurate for languages and misleading for communities, since a community can change its language and a language can outlive the community that named it.

## [ne] भाषा जनसङ्ख्या होइन

भाषाको नक्सा खोल्नुहोस्, गुरुङ ग्लोटोलगको वर्गीकरणअनुसार चिनियाँ-तिब्बती परिवारभित्र, तामाङ र थकालीको छेउमा पर्छ। त्यो नक्सा भाषाहरूबीचको नाताको चित्र हो, र यसलाई जनताहरूबीचको नाताको चित्र ठान्नु सजिलो छ।

त्यो होइन। भाषा परिवारले भन्छ, यी भाषा कुनै समयमा एउटै पुर्खाबाट आएका हुन्, र पुर्खा भाषा बोल्नेहरू कुनै बिन्दुमा सम्पर्कमा थिए वा एउटै जनता थिए — त्यो पनि कसैले केही लेख्नुभन्दा धेरै अघि। यसले भन्ने छैन ती वक्ता कहाँ बस्थे, कहिले छुट्टिए, वा आज कोही त्यही पुर्खा बोल्ने जनताको सिधा सन्तान हो। भाषा जनसङ्ख्याभर फैलिन सक्छ, त्यसलाई प्रतिस्थापन नगरी, र जनसङ्ख्या नसर्दै भाषा बदल्न सक्छ।

नक्साले ठ्याक्कै त्यही गल्ती निम्त्याउँछ, त्यसैले यो अध्यायको पहिलो काम त्यसलाई अस्वीकार गर्नु हो। उत्पत्ति खोज्ने गुरुङ पाठकले वंशवृक्ष भेट्छ, र वृक्ष साँच्चै छ; जो छैन त्यो वृक्षको हाँगाबाट लमजुङको गाउँसम्मको सिधा रेखा हो। त्यसैले अर्को प्रश्न "वृक्षमा कहाँ" होइन, "कस्तो प्रमाणले थप भन्न सक्छ" हो।

यो भूल यति सामान्य छ कि यसको आकार छ। पाठकले गुरुङ तामाङ र थकालीको छेउमा देख्छ, तीनै कुनै बेला एउटै जनता थिए भन्ने निष्कर्ष निकाल्छ, र त्यसपछि त्यो जनता बसेको उपत्यका खोज्छ। नेपालका भाषिक सर्वेक्षणले पनि परिवारलाई हाँगा भएको वृक्षका रूपमा देखाएर त्यही भूललाई प्रोत्साहन दिन्छन्, जो भाषाका लागि सही र समुदायका लागि भ्रामक छ, किनकि समुदायले आफ्नो भाषा बदल्न सक्छ र भाषा आफ्नो नाम राख्ने समुदायभन्दा लामो बाँच्न सक्छ।

## [en] Different kinds of evidence

Around Kohla, Christopher Evans combined archaeological investigation with community ethnohistory rather than treating one as a substitute for the other. That combination is rarer than it sounds: archaeologists usually excavate and ethnographers usually talk, and the two literatures rarely meet, partly because they answer questions on different scales.

Each kind of evidence answers a different question. Archaeology can date a wall and say what was eaten and what was traded. Historical linguistics can say roughly when two languages parted company. Oral narratives can say what a community believes about its own past, and why that belief matters enough to be recited. Population genetics can trace ancestry across continents over thousands of years.

Each also has its own characteristic error. A radiocarbon date can come from a disturbed layer. A loanword can have been borrowed twice, or borrowed back. A genealogy can be polished for prestige, or shortened to hide a servile origin. A genetic sample can be too small, or drawn from a group that is not representative. None of these errors is a reason to discard the evidence; each is a reason to keep the kinds apart and to say which one is speaking.

What would a good origin claim look like? It would need a dated site, a reconstructed vocabulary with a plausible homeland, a genetic sample large enough to mean something, and an oral tradition recorded independently in several places. Those four would have to agree. Nothing in the current literature assembles them, which is why this chapter describes the state of the question rather than answering it.

The distinction between a hypothesis and a finding is what a reader needs to carry away. A hypothesis can be interesting, useful and wrong; a finding has survived an attempt to disprove it. Most of what circulates about Gurung origins is hypothesis presented as finding, and the correction is not to replace it with a different certainty but to restore the label.

## [ne] फरक प्रमाणका किसिम

कोह्ला आसपास Christopher Evans ले पुरातात्त्विक अन्वेषणलाई सामुदायिक नृवंश-इतिहाससँग जोडे, एउटालाई अर्कोको विकल्प बनाइन। त्यो संयोजन सुन्दा जति सामान्य छ, त्यति छैन: पुरातत्त्वविद्ले खन्छन्, नृवंशविद्ले कुरा गर्छन्, र दुई साहित्य विरलै भेट्छन्, किनकि तिनले फरक मापनमा प्रश्नको जवाफ दिन्छन्।

हरेक किसिमको प्रमाणले फरक प्रश्नको जवाफ दिन्छ। पुरातत्त्वले पर्खालको मिति तोक्न सक्छ र के खाइन्थ्यो, के व्यापार हुन्थ्यो भन्न सक्छ। ऐतिहासिक भाषाविज्ञानले दुई भाषा कहिले छुट्टिए भन्ने लगभग बताउन सक्छ। मौखिक कथाले समुदायले आफ्नो विगतबारे के विश्वास गर्छ र त्यो विश्वास किन यति महत्त्वपूर्ण छ कि वाचन गरिन्छ भन्न सक्छ। जनसङ्ख्या आनुवंशिकीले हजारौँ वर्षमा महादेश पार गर्दै पुर्ख्यौली पछ्याउन सक्छ।

हरेकका आफ्नै किसिमका त्रुटि पनि छन्। रेडियोकार्बन मिति बिग्रिएको तहबाट आउन सक्छ। सापटी शब्द दुईचोटि सापटी लिइएको हुन सक्छ, वा फर्काएर लिइएको। वंशावली प्रतिष्ठाका लागि चम्काइएको वा दास-उत्पत्ति लुकाउन छोट्याइएको हुन सक्छ। आनुवंशिक नमुना सानो वा प्रतिनिधित्व नगर्ने समूहबाट लिइएको हुन सक्छ। यीमध्ये कुनै पनि त्रुटि प्रमाण फाल्ने कारण होइन; हरेक किसिम अलग राख्ने र कुन बोलिरहेको छ भन्ने कारण हो।

राम्रो उत्पत्ति-दाबी कस्तो देखिन्थ्यो? त्यसलाई मिति भएको स्थल, सम्भाव्य मातृभूमिसहित पुनर्निर्मित शब्दभण्डार, अर्थपूर्ण हुने गरी ठूलो आनुवंशिक नमुना, र धेरै ठाउँमा स्वतन्त्र रूपमा रेकर्ड भएको मौखिक परम्परा चाहिन्थ्यो। ती चारै मिल्नुपर्थ्यो। अहिलेको साहित्यमा कुनैले पनि तिनलाई जोड्दैन, त्यसैले यो अध्याय प्रश्नको जवाफ होइन, प्रश्नको अवस्था वर्णन गर्छ।

परिकल्पना र निष्कर्षबीचको भेद पाठकले घर लैजानुपर्ने कुरा हो। परिकल्पना रोचक, उपयोगी र गलत हुन सक्छ; निष्कर्ष खण्डन गर्ने प्रयासबाट बाँचेको हुन्छ। गुरुङ उत्पत्तिबारे घुम्ने धेरै कुरा निष्कर्षका रूपमा प्रस्तुत परिकल्पना हो, र सुधार भनेको अर्को निश्चितता राख्नु होइन, लेबल फर्काउनु हो।

## [en] What remains unresolved

A precise ancient homeland, a single migration date and direct descent from a named ancient population are not established by the sources presented here.

Claims of a Yellow River connection, a Tibetan Plateau origin or descent from the Qiang are repeated in popular accounts, and some of them may have substance. Each requires its own critical research: a dated archaeological sequence, a linguistic reconstruction that fits, a genetic study with adequate sampling, an oral tradition recorded in several places and compared. None of that has been assembled, and repeating the claims as settled Gurung genealogy gives them a certainty they do not have.

The temptation to settle it is strong, because a community that has been told for two centuries that it came from somewhere else wants to know where. There is also a political edge to the question, since origin claims are used to argue about who is indigenous to which land. The honest answer is that the question is open, and the reason is not caution but the state of the ground — which is the next thing to look at.

Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence, and this chapter does not claim that the Gurung have no ancient history. It claims something narrower and more useful: that the specific propositions in circulation — a named homeland, a single migration, descent from a named ancient population — have not been demonstrated. A reader is free to believe them; a reader is not free to call them established.

प्रमाणको अभाव अभावको प्रमाण होइन, र यो अध्यायले गुरुङको प्राचीन इतिहास छैन भन्दैन। यसले अझ साँघुरो र उपयोगी कुरा भन्छ: घुमिरहेका खास दाबी — नाम खुलेको मातृभूमि, एउटै बसाइँसराइ, नाम खुलेको प्राचीन जनसमूहबाट सिधा वंश — प्रमाणित भएका छैनन्। पाठक स्वतन्त्र छ ती मान्न, तर तिनलाई स्थापित भन्न स्वतन्त्र छैन।

## [en] The archaeological silence

No one has excavated a Gurung capital. There are no inscribed royal archives, no sealed tomb, no cemetery sequence published with radiocarbon dates that could anchor a chronology — the kinds of find that let an archaeologist say "this place was occupied from this century to that one".

What exists is scattered and suggestive. Stone structures and terraces whose dates are uncertain. Place names that may preserve older settlement patterns. Comparisons with neighbouring regions where more work has been done, and where a sequence established in one valley can be used cautiously to interpret another. None of it adds up to a founding date.

The silence is not evidence that nothing happened. It reflects where research money and archaeological attention have gone, and the fact that hill settlements are hard to excavate and easy to disturb: a terraced slope has been dug over for centuries, stone is reused, and the oldest layers are usually gone before anyone looks. Any account of origins that claims certainty is overreaching, and the reader should treat it as such. Where the ground is silent, the community's own recitation speaks.

Hill archaeology is expensive in a particular way. A site has to be reached, which means days of walking or a helicopter; it has to be protected while it is open; and it has to be published, which takes years after the trench is closed. Terracing, stone robbing and centuries of manuring have removed the layers that would answer the question. That is why the record is thin, and why the thinness says more about research than about the past.

There is also a quieter reason for the silence: nobody has looked very hard. The archaeology of the middle hills has been funded by tourism, by conservation and by curiosity about the great monuments of the Kathmandu valley, and a terrace above a village has never competed well for a grant. When the research priority changes, the evidence may change with it.

## [ne] पुरातात्त्विक मौनता

कसैले गुरुङ राजधानी खनेको छैन। शिलालेख भएको राजकीय अभिलेख छैन, सिल गरिएको चिहान छैन, रेडियोकार्बन मितिसहित प्रकाशित चिहान-क्रम छैन — त्यस्ता फेला जसले पुरातत्त्वविद्लाई "यो ठाउँ यो शताब्दीदेखि उससम्म बसोबास थियो" भन्न दिन्छ।

जो छ, छरिएको र सङ्केत गर्ने छ: मिति अनिश्चित ढुङ्गाका संरचना र आँगन, पुरानो बस्तीको ढाँचा जोगाउन सक्ने ठाउँका नाम, र बढी काम भएका छिमेकी क्षेत्रसँगको तुलना, जहाँ एउटा उपत्यकामा स्थापित क्रम अर्कोलाई होसियारीसाथ बुझ्न प्रयोग गर्न सकिन्छ। यसले स्थापना-मिति दिँदैन।

मौनता केही भएन भन्ने प्रमाण होइन। यसले अनुसन्धानको पैसा र पुरातात्त्विक ध्यान कहाँ गयो देखाउँछ, र पहाडी बस्ती खन्न गाह्रो र बिगार्न सजिलो हुन्छ भन्ने पनि: सीढीदार भिरालो शताब्दीदेखि खनिएको छ, ढुङ्गा फेरि प्रयोग भएको छ, र सबैभन्दा पुराना तह कसैले हेर्नुअघि नै प्रायः गइसकेका हुन्छन्। निश्चितता दाबी गर्ने कुनै पनि उत्पत्ति-कथा बढी दाबी गरिरहेको हुन्छ, र पाठकले त्यसलाई त्यसै गरी लिनुपर्छ। जमिन मौन हुँदा समुदायको आफ्नै वाचन बोल्छ।

पहाडको पुरातत्त्व महँगो छ, एउटा खास तरिकाले। स्थलसम्म पुग्नुपर्छ, जसको अर्थ दिनौँको हिँडाइ वा हेलिकप्टर; खुला रहँदा सुरक्षा चाहिन्छ; र खाडल पुर्सकेपछि प्रकाशन गर्न वर्षौँ लाग्छ। सीढी बनाउने, ढुङ्गा लैजाने र शताब्दीको मलले प्रश्नको जवाफ दिने तह हटाइसकेका छन्। त्यसैले अभिलेख पातलो छ, र त्यो पातलोपन विगतभन्दा अनुसन्धानबारे बढी भन्छ।

मौनताको एउटा शान्त कारण पनि छ: कसैले धेरै खोजेको छैन। मध्य पहाडको पुरातत्त्वलाई पर्यटन, संरक्षण र काठमाडौँ उपत्यकाका ठूला सम्पदाप्रतिको जिज्ञासाले पैसा दिएको छ, र गाउँमाथिको आँगनले अनुदानका लागि कहिल्यै राम्रोसँग प्रतिस्पर्धा गरेन। जब अनुसन्धानको प्राथमिकता बदलिन्छ, प्रमाण पनि बदलिन सक्छ।

## [en] What the pye can and cannot say

In a specialist's recitation, the pye carries the community's own account of where it came from. It names places, describes migrations and lists the ancestors who founded clans and settlements. It is performed in a fixed language, by named practitioners, before an audience that knows the story and will notice a mistake. It is the most important indigenous historical source there is.

But a pye is a ritual text, not a chronicle. It was transmitted orally, and oral transmission preserves what matters to the rite rather than what would satisfy a historian. It varies between specialists, because two teachers in two valleys learned it from different predecessors. It compresses events into poetry, because that is how it is remembered. And it cares about legitimacy as much as sequence: a genealogy in a pye is also a claim about who has the right to stand where.

A place name in a pye may therefore be a real location, a mythical one, or a real place invested with a story that has moved with the people who tell it. Reading such texts historically means comparing versions, comparing them with the landscape and with other sources, and accepting probabilities instead of dates. That is a slower and less satisfying method than quoting a line as though it were a chronicle, and it is the only one that does not turn a poem into a false document.

Comparing versions is the method, and it is slow. A researcher records the same passage from several specialists, notes where they diverge, and asks whether the divergence follows the landscape, the clan or the teacher. Where two versions agree on a place that can be found today, that agreement is evidence of a kind; where they disagree, the disagreement is often the more interesting document.

## [ne] प्येले भन्न सक्ने र नसक्ने

विशेषज्ञको वाचनमा प्येले समुदायको आफ्नै उत्पत्ति-विवरण बोक्छ। तिनले ठाउँको नाम लिन्छन्, बसाइँसराइ वर्णन गर्छन् र क्ले तथा बस्ती स्थापना गर्ने पुर्खाको सूची दिन्छन्। यो स्थिर भाषामा, नाम खुलेका विशेषज्ञद्वारा, कथा जान्ने र गल्ती चिन्ने दर्शकसामु वाचन गरिन्छ। यो उपलब्ध सबैभन्दा महत्त्वपूर्ण स्वदेशी ऐतिहासिक स्रोत हो।

तर प्ये कर्मकाण्डी पाठ हो, इतिहास-पुस्तक होइन। यो मौखिक रूपमा सरेको छ, र मौखिक सरणले कर्मलाई महत्त्वपूर्ण कुरा जोगाउँछ, इतिहासकारलाई चित्त बुझाउने कुरा होइन। यो विशेषज्ञअनुसार फरक पर्छ, किनकि दुई उपत्यकाका दुई गुरुले फरक पूर्ववर्तीबाट सिके। यो घटनालाई कवितामा खुम्च्याउँछ, किनकि त्यसरी नै सम्झिन्छ। र यसलाई क्रमजत्तिकै वैधताको चासो छ: प्येको वंशावली कसको कहाँ उभिने हक छ भन्ने दाबी पनि हो।

त्यसैले प्येमा आउने ठाउँको नाम वास्तविक स्थान, पौराणिक स्थान, वा कथा सरेर आएको वास्तविक ठाउँ हुन सक्छ। त्यस्ता पाठलाई ऐतिहासिक रूपमा पढ्न संस्करणहरूबीच, भूदृश्यसँग र अरू स्रोतसँग तुलना चाहिन्छ, र त्यसले मिति होइन, सम्भावना दिन्छ। यो कुनै पंक्ति इतिहास-पुस्तक झैँ उद्धृत गर्नुभन्दा ढिलो र कम सन्तोषजनक विधि हो, र कवितालाई झुटो कागजमा नबदल्ने एउटै विधि पनि।

संस्करण तुलना गर्नु नै विधि हो, र त्यो ढिलो छ। अनुसन्धानकर्ताले एउटै अनुच्छेद धेरै विशेषज्ञबाट रेकर्ड गर्छ, कहाँ फरक पर्छ टिप्छ, र त्यो फरक भूदृश्य, क्ले वा गुरुको पछि लाग्छ कि भनेर सोध्छ। जहाँ दुई संस्करण आज भेटिने ठाउँमा मिल्छन्, त्यो मिलाइ एक किसिमको प्रमाण हो; जहाँ फरक पर्छन्, त्यो असहमति प्रायः बढी रोचक कागज हुन्छ।

## [en] Dating a migration without documents

Ask three specialists when the Gurung reached Nepal and you get three kinds of answer.

Glottochronology — the attempt to date the split between related languages by counting how much vocabulary they share — and the comparative study of the Tamangic languages suggest a separation measured in centuries or more, with all the error that such estimates carry. The study of loanwords can bracket contact with Tibetan and Nepali, because a word borrowed at a known period dates the contact. Place names and land grants give termini ante quos: a village named in a grant of a certain year was there by that year, whatever happened before.

None of them yields a date for "the Gurung arrival", because there was probably no single arrival. The community formed through a long process of movement, intermarriage and political incorporation, and a process has no birthday. Asking for one is like asking when a river became a river.

The useful question is therefore not when the Gurung reached Nepal but how the groups that became Gurung came to share a language, a set of clans and a story about themselves. That question can be answered in parts, and the next chapter follows one of those parts: the movement itself, as the community remembers it and as the landscape records it.

Relative and absolute dating answer different questions. A sequence can tell you that a wall is older than a floor without telling you what year either was built; a single radiocarbon date can anchor a sequence but only if the sample is clean. The Gurung past has plenty of sequence and almost no anchors, which is why the dating question keeps returning and why the next chapter turns to movement instead.

The distinction matters for how the rest of the book is read. Chapters that follow describe clans, villages and rituals that can be dated in decades; this one describes a depth of time that can only be dated in centuries, and the two should not be confused when a reader puts the book down and repeats what it says.

## [ne] दस्तावेजबिना बसाइँसराइको मिति

तीन विशेषज्ञलाई गुरुङ नेपाल कहिले आए भनेर सोध्नुहोस्, तीन किसिमका जवाफ आउँछन्।

ग्लोटोक्रोनोलजी — सम्बन्धित भाषाबीचको फाटो कति शब्द साझा छन् गनेर मिति तोक्ने प्रयास — र तामाङ्गिक भाषाहरूको तुलनात्मक अध्ययनले शताब्दी वा बढीको पृथकीकरण देखाउँछ, त्यस्ता अनुमानले बोक्ने त्रुटिसहित। सापटी शब्दको अध्ययनले तिब्बती र नेपालीसँगको सम्पर्कको समय-सीमा बाँध्न सक्छ, किनकि ज्ञात अवधिमा सापटी लिइएको शब्दले त्यो सम्पर्कको मिति तोक्छ। ठाउँका नाम र जग्गा-दानले मिति-पूर्व सीमा दिन्छ: कुनै दानपत्रको वर्षमा नाम लिइएको गाउँ त्यो वर्षसम्म थियो, अघि के भयो भए पनि।

यीमध्ये कुनैले "गुरुङ आगमन" को मिति दिँदैन, किनकि एउटै आगमन थिएन होला। समुदाय आवागमन, अन्तरविवाह र राजनीतिक समावेशीकरणको लामो प्रक्रियाबाट बन्यो, र प्रक्रियाको जन्मदिन हुँदैन। त्यसो सोध्नु नदी कहिले नदी भयो भनेर सोध्नुजस्तो हो।

त्यसैले उपयोगी प्रश्न — गुरुङ नेपाल कहिले आए होइन, गुरुङ बनेका समूहले भाषा, क्ले-समूह र आफूबारेको कथा कसरी साझा गर्न थाले भन्ने हो। त्यो प्रश्न भागमा जवाफ दिन सकिन्छ, र अर्को अध्याय तीमध्ये एउटा भाग पछ्याउँछ: आवागमन, जसरी समुदायले सम्झिन्छ र भूदृश्यले अभिलेख गर्छ।

सापेक्ष र निरपेक्ष मिति फरक प्रश्नको जवाफ दिन्छन्। क्रमले पर्खाल भुइँभन्दा पुरानो हो भन्न सक्छ, कुनै वर्ष कहिले बने भन्न नसकी; एउटै रेडियोकार्बन मितिले क्रम बाँध्न सक्छ, तर नमुना सफा भए मात्र। गुरुङ विगतमा क्रम प्रशस्त छ र बाँध्ने खम्बा लगभग छैन, त्यसैले मितिको प्रश्न फर्किरहन्छ र अर्को अध्याय आवागमनतिर फर्कन्छ।

यो भेद पुस्तकको बाँकी भाग कसरी पढ्ने भन्नेसँग पनि जोडिन्छ। अबका अध्यायले क्ले, गाउँ र कर्मको वर्णन गर्छन् जो दशकमा मिति तोक्न सकिन्छ; यसले शताब्दीमा मात्र तोकिने समय-गहिराइ वर्णन गर्छ, र पाठकले पुस्तक बन्द गरेर यसले भनेको दोहोर्‍याउँदा दुईलाई मिसाउनु हुँदैन।
