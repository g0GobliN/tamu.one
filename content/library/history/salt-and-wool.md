## [en] Two commodities, one road

The Himalayan trade is often reduced to salt, and salt was indeed the commodity that made the routes pay: Tibetan rock salt, pressed into bricks or carried in bags, was the only affordable salt for much of the middle hills until the twentieth century. But the caravans carried a whole basket. Wool from Tibetan sheep went south to be woven into blankets and pakhi; butter and dried cheese travelled with it; hides, musk and medicinal plants came down; grain, chillies, pulses, iron implements, cotton cloth and paper went up. A single caravan could be a moving market, and the villages along the route were its customers as well as its hosts.

## [ne] दुई वस्तु, एउटा बाटो

हिमाली व्यापारलाई प्रायः नुनमा सीमित गरिन्छ, र नुनले नै मार्गलाई फाइदाजनक बनाएको थियो: इँटामा थिचिएको वा बोरामा बोकिएको तिब्बती ढुङ्गा-नुन बीसौँ शताब्दीसम्म मध्य पहाडका धेरै ठाउँका लागि एकमात्र सुलभ नुन थियो। तर काफिलाले पूरै टोकरी बोक्थे। तिब्बती भेडाको ऊन दक्षिण आउँथ्यो, कम्बल र पाखी बुनिन्थ्यो; घ्यू र सुकाएको छुरा सँगै आउँथे; छाला, कस्तुरी र जडीबुटी तल झर्थे; अन्न, खुर्सानी, दाल, फलामका औजार, सुती कपडा र कागज माथि जान्थ्यो। एउटै काफिला हिँड्दो बजार हुन सक्थ्यो, र मार्गका गाउँ तिनका ग्राहक पनि थिए, आयोजक पनि।

## [en] Kerong and the northern door

For the Gurung areas, the most important northern door was Kyirong, the valley and trading town that Nepalis call Kerong, reached from the Langtang and Trisuli side and connected to the Kathmandu valley through Nuwakot. Goods from Lhasa came through Kyirong; goods from the Nepali hills went up to it. A second corridor ran through Mustang and the Kali Gandaki, and a third through Manang and the Marsyangdi. Control of these doors was worth fighting over, and the hill states that held them taxed the traffic. When Prithvi Narayan Shah took Nuwakot in 1744 and then the Kathmandu valley in 1768, he was securing a trade system as much as a territory.

## [ne] क्यिरोङ र उत्तरी ढोका

गुरुङ क्षेत्रका लागि सबैभन्दा महत्त्वपूर्ण उत्तरी ढोका क्यिरोङ थियो — नेपालीमा केरोङ भनिने उपत्यका र व्यापार-सहर, लाङटाङ र त्रिशूलीतिरबाट पुगिने र नुवाकोट हुँदै काठमाडौँ उपत्यकासँग जोडिने। ल्हासाका सामान क्यिरोङ हुँदै आउँथे; नेपाली पहाडका सामान त्यहीँ माथि जान्थे। दोस्रो कोरिडोर मुस्ताङ र कालीगण्डकी हुँदै, तेस्रो मनाङ र मर्स्याङ्दी हुँदै चल्थ्यो। यी ढोकाको नियन्त्रण लडाइँको विषय थियो, र ती राख्ने पहाडी राज्यले आवागमनमा कर लाउँथे। पृथ्वीनारायण शाहले १७४४ मा नुवाकोट र १७६८ मा काठमाडौँ उपत्यका लिँदा भूभागजत्तिकै व्यापार-व्यवस्था पनि सुरक्षित गर्दै थिए।

## [en] The treaty centuries

War and trade ran together. The Nepal–Tibet war of 1788–92 ended with the Treaty of Betrawati, which required Nepal to return captured property and allowed trade to resume under terms; the Anglo-Nepal war of 1814–16 cost Nepal the plains but left the northern trade intact. The Thapathali Treaty of 1856, imposed after another war with Tibet, gave Nepal trading privileges and a resident in Lhasa, and for the next century Nepali traders — including Gurung and other hill men — operated in Tibet under a protected status. The treaty system made the caravans predictable, and predictability is what allowed a household to plan a year around a journey.

## [ne] सन्धिका शताब्दी

युद्ध र व्यापार सँगै चले। १७८८–९२ को नेपाल–तिब्बत युद्ध बेत्रावती सन्धिमा टुङ्गियो, जसले नेपालले लिएको सम्पत्ति फिर्ता गर्न र सर्तअनुसार व्यापार फेरि सुरु गर्न भन्यो; १८१४–१६ को अङ्ग्रेज-नेपाल युद्धले नेपाललाई तराई गुमायो तर उत्तरी व्यापार जोगियो। तिब्बतसँगको अर्को युद्धपछि लादिएको १८५६ को थापाथली सन्धिले नेपाललाई व्यापारिक सुविधा र ल्हासामा नेपाली प्रतिनिधि दियो, र अर्को शताब्दी नेपाली व्यापारी — गुरुङलगायत पहाडी मानिस — संरक्षित दर्जामा तिब्बतमा चले। सन्धि-व्यवस्थाले काफिलालाई पूर्वानुमानयोग्य बनायो, र त्यही पूर्वानुमानले घरलाई वर्षभरिको यात्रा योजना गर्न दियो।

## [en] Caravans and cattle

A caravan was an organisation, not a crowd. It had a leader, a route, a schedule and a division of labour: drivers, loaders, cooks, men who knew the passes and men who knew the customs posts. Mules were preferred where the track was good; yaks and dzo took the high snow; goats and sheep were both cargo and food. The animals had to be fed, which meant grazing grounds and arrangements with villages along the way. In the Gurung hills, the same households that farmed terraces also kept herds and moved them to summer pastures, so the skills of the caravan and the skills of the herder overlapped. When the trade ended, those skills did not disappear at once; they were redirected into army service, portering and labour migration.

## [ne] काफिला र पशु

काफिला भीड होइन, संगठन थियो। यसको नेता, मार्ग, तालिका र कामको बाँडफाँट हुन्थ्यो: चालक, भार बोक्ने, पकाउने, भञ्ज्याङ चिन्ने र भन्सार चौकी चिन्ने मानिस। बाटो राम्रो भए खच्चर रुचाइन्थ्यो; हिउँमा याक र द्जो जान्थे; बाख्रा र भेडा भार पनि, खाना पनि। पशुलाई खुवाउनु पर्थ्यो, त्यसैले बाटोका चरन र गाउँसँग व्यवस्था चाहिन्थ्यो। गुरुङ पहाडमा आँगन खेती गर्ने घरले नै बथान राख्थे र गर्मी चरनमा सार्थे, त्यसैले काफिलाको सीप र गोठालोको सीप एकअर्कामा मिसिएका थिए। व्यापार सकिँदा ती सीप एकैचोटि हराएनन्; ती सेवा, भरिया काम र श्रम-बसाइँसराइतिर मोडिए।

## [en] The road replaced the pass

The collapse came in stages: the Chinese presence in Tibet after 1950, the closure of the border after 1959, the 1962 war, and then the roads. The Prithvi Highway and later the Kathmandu–Pokhara highway brought cheap manufactured salt and goods from the south, and the mule caravans could not compete. Villages that had lived from lodging and fodder lost that income; families that had lived from trade turned to soldiering, to government service and eventually to the Gulf. What remains is the memory of the route, a handful of trading families with stories of Lhasa, and the place names that still mark where the caravans stopped.
